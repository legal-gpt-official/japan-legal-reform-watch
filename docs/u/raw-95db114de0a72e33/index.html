The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) has opened a public comment period on a draft notice that would partially amend certain existing MAFF notices, including Notice No. 1184 of 2002 issued under Article 1, item 9 of the enforcement order of the Agricultural Modernization Fund Financing Act.
