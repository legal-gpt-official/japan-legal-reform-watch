Summary

The Basel Committee on Banking Supervision published proposed additional Pillar 2 guidance on interest rate risk in the banking book (IRRBB) on 2026-11-09; the Financial Services Agency announced it on 2026-11-16. The proposal addresses banks and supervisory authorities and aims to strengthen the effectiveness of the 2016 standards in light of risk-management challenges exposed by inflation, sharp interest-rate increases and heightened market volatility. It covers model risk management and validation, stress testing, IRRBB internal controls, treatment of unrealized gains and losses on securities portfolios, short- and medium-term earnings measures, and supervisory information. Revisions to the standards are also proposed. The stated deadline for comments in English to the Basel Committee is 2027-02-09.

Business Impact

Banks may need to assess how the proposed guidance compares with their IRRBB models, stress testing, internal controls and treatment of securities-portfolio valuation changes. The excerpt describes a consultation, not a confirmed new obligation or implementation timetable.

Recommended Action

Banks could review the consultation's proposed model-validation, earnings-measurement and unrealized-gain/loss provisions against their IRRBB practices, and note the stated 2027-02-09 comment deadline.
