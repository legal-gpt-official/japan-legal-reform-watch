Summary

Cabinet Order No. 385 of 2024 provides for amendments to related Cabinet Orders and transitional measures accompanying implementation of the Act on payment of compensation to persons who underwent eugenic surgery under the former Eugenic Protection Act and others. The listed amendment concerns the Enforcement Order of the Act on Appropriate Execution of the Budget Pertaining to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955); the stated promulgation date is 2024-12-18 and the effective date is 2025-01-17.

Business Impact
