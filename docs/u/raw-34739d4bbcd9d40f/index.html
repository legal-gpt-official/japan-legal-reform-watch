Summary

Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry Ordinance No. 72 of 2026 amends rules on high-pressure gas production safety manager examinations under the High Pressure Gas Safety Act and the enforcement regulations under the Act on Securing Safety and Proper Transactions of Liquefied Petroleum Gas. The latter regulations are Ministry of International Trade and Industry Ordinance No. 11 of 1997. The metadata states a promulgation date of 2026-09-30 and an effective date of 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

The amendments may affect candidates for high-pressure gas production safety manager examinations and liquefied petroleum gas businesses governed by the enforcement regulations, although the specific changes are not described.

Recommended Action

Reviewing the official Japanese ordinance could clarify the amended examination provisions, changes to the LPG enforcement regulations, and any transitional arrangements.

Background & Purpose

Paper-style certificates and older application formats created an administrative obstacle to plastic-card issuance and reuse of photographs already submitted with examination applications. The Ministry's consultation identified certificate-format modernization and procedural consistency as direct objectives.

The package also reflects revised industry technical standards for seismic evaluation and composite gas cylinders. These technical-reference changes are distinct from the certificate rules and should not be conflated with a wholesale change to plant operating permits.

Legislative & Regulatory History

METI opened consultation on 22 July 2026, explaining proposed plastic-card issuance for gas safety qualifications, use of examination photographs, reissuance after name changes and updated technical-standard references.

The ordinance was promulgated on 30 September 2026 and the e-Gov law history records its entry into force on 1 October 2026. The associated technical notices and interpretations must be checked on their own terms rather than assumed to have identical legal status.

Business Impact & Practical Considerations

Applicants and certificate issuers may use updated processes for plastic-card credentials, photograph handling and reissuance. Whether a particular certificate is issued in a given format depends on the implementing procedures, not merely the fact of promulgation.

Engineering and safety teams should separately review the updated seismic and cylinder standards referenced by technical notices when designing or inspecting affected equipment. The ordinance alone should not be described as changing every technical standard for high-pressure gas installations.

Points to check

  • Check the issuing body's current application, photograph and name-change reissuance instructions for the relevant gas qualification.
  • Identify which seismic or cylinder standards are cited in the facility's design basis and check separately issued METI interpretations.
