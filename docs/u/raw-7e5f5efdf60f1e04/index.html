Summary

This is a Cabinet Order (Reiwa 8 Cabinet Order No. 85) providing for the arrangement of related Cabinet Orders in connection with the enforcement of a law partially amending the Customs Tariff Act and related laws. According to the provided metadata, it affects the Enforcement Order for the Law on Processing of Import/Export-Related Operations by Electronic Data Processing Systems (Showa 52 Cabinet Order No. 220). The metadata lists a promulgation date of 2026-03-31 and an enforcement date of 2026-04-01.

Business Impact

Businesses involved in customs, import/export operations, or electronic processing of trade-related procedures may be affected by adjustments to the relevant Cabinet Orders.
