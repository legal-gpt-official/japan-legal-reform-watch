Summary

This item concerns a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare ordinance (Reiwa 8 MHLW Ordinance No. 116) that arranges related ministerial regulations in connection with the partial enforcement of amendments to the Industrial Safety and Health Act and the Working Environment Measurement Act. According to the metadata, it affects the Ordinance on Prevention of Lead Poisoning (Showa 47 Labour Ministry Ordinance No. 37). The metadata indicates a promulgation date of 2026-07-29 and an enforcement date of 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

Organizations subject to occupational safety and health or lead poisoning prevention rules may be affected by changes to the applicable regulations and could need to review their obligations.
