Summary

Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) has published the results of a public comment process concerning a draft notification (kokuji) that would partially amend technical conditions for specified low-power radio stations. The draft addresses items such as devices not required to be housed in a single enclosure, transmission time limiting devices, and carrier sense requirements. This item reflects the outcome of the consultation; the metadata does not confirm whether the amendment has been finalized or has taken effect.

Business Impact

Organizations that design, manufacture, or operate specified low-power radio equipment may need to consider whether the proposed technical conditions could affect product compliance.
