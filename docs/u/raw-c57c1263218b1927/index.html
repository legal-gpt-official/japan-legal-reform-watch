Summary

The Ministry of Finance published a draft Cabinet Order for public comment on 2026-08-20. The draft would amend the Order for Enforcement of the National Public Service Mutual Aid Association Act and an earlier amending Cabinet Order concerning that enforcement order and transitional measures for long-term benefits associated with pension unification and retirement-benefit reforms. The stated comment deadline is 2026/09/19 at 00:00.
