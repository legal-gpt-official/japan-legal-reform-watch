Summary

Ministry of Finance Ordinance No. 63 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-10-01, with an effective date of 2027-03-31. It amends the ordinance restricting payment using securities under Article 6(1) of Imperial Ordinance No. 256 of 1916 and the Detailed Enforcement Rules for the Act on Payment of Public Revenues Using Securities (Ministry of Finance Ordinance No. 32 of 1916).
