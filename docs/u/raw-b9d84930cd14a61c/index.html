Summary

Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) has published a request for public comments on a draft revision of the guideline concerning Article 26 of the law on responding to rights infringements arising from the distribution of information via specified telecommunications. This is a draft (revision proposal) out for public comment, not a finalized or enacted measure. The official Japanese source on the MIC website is authoritative.

Business Impact

Operators of telecommunications or online platforms handling user-distributed information may wish to note that this draft could affect how rights-infringement responses under Article 26 are interpreted. Any impact would depend on the final adopted text.
