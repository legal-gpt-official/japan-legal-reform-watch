Summary

The Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) announced the results of a public comment procedure on a draft partial amendment to the notice setting eligibility requirements for the Class-A Fire Protection Equipment Officer (kōshu shōbō setsubi-shi) examination, together with the promulgation of the amended notice. The item indicates both that a consultation on the draft has concluded and that an amending notice has been promulgated. Specific details of the eligibility changes are not provided in the metadata.

Business Impact

Parties involved in fire protection equipment work or preparing for the relevant qualification exam may be affected if the eligibility requirements have changed. Training providers and employers could need to reassess how candidates qualify for the examination.

Recommended Action

Review the official MIC source and the promulgated amended notice to confirm the specific eligibility changes and their timing.
