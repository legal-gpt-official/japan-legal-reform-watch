Summary

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 72 of 2026 amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Farmland Act and the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on Promoting Electricity Generation from Renewable Energy Harmonized with Sound Development of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries. The latter regulations are Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 33 of 2014. The stated promulgation and effective dates are both 2026-10-09.

Business Impact

Farmland users and renewable electricity generation operators whose projects concern agriculture, forestry or fisheries may be affected by changes to the implementing rules. The supplied information does not identify the amended provisions or their operational effects.

Recommended Action

Reviewing the official Japanese source would help identify the amended provisions in both sets of regulations and any transitional arrangements.
