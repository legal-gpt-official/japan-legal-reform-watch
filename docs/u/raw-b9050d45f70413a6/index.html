Summary

This item is an amending ministerial ordinance to the ordinance (Heisei 6 MITI Ordinance No. 36) that designates foreign national flags, emblems, official supervisory or certification seals of foreign governments or local authorities, and marks of international organizations under Articles 16(1), 16(3), and 17 of Japan's Unfair Competition Prevention Act.
