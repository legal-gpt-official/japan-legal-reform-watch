Summary

This is a draft ministerial ordinance out for public comment concerning the certification of persons who carry out support operations for businesses adapting to reduced demand for essential goods and services, as provided under Article 140-28, Paragraph 1 of the Industrial Competitiveness Enhancement Act. According to the metadata, the draft was published on 2026-09-08 with a comment submission deadline of 2026-10-07 23:59.
