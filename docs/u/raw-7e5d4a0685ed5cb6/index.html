Summary

This is a Cabinet Order (Reiwa 7 Cabinet Order No. 341), promulgated 2025-10-01, that prepares related ordinances and sets transitional measures accompanying the entry into force of an amendment to the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Technical Intern Training Act. The e-Gov update listing indicates it amends the Enforcement Order of the Act on Employment Promotion etc. of Persons with Disabilities (Showa 35 Cabinet Order No. 292), with an indicated effective date of 2027-04-01.
