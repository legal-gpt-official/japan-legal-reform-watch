摘要

2026年第24号防卫省省令于2026-09-30公布，并于2026-10-01生效，配合《防卫省设置法等部分修正法》的部分施行，对相关省令进行调整。所列修订涉及2012年第6号防卫省省令，该省令规定了《国家公务员工资调整及临时特例措施法》施行后自卫队人员等平均工资计算的特别规则。

Summary

Ministry of Defense Ordinance No. 24 of 2026, promulgated on 2026-09-30 and effective on 2026-10-01, adjusts related ministerial ordinances in connection with partial implementation of the Act Partially Amending the Ministry of Defense Establishment Act and Other Acts. The listed amendment concerns Ministry of Defense Ordinance No. 6 of 2012, which establishes special rules for calculating average pay for Self-Defense Forces personnel and others following implementation of the Act on Revision and Temporary Special Measures for National Public Employees' Pay.
