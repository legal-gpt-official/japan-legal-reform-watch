Summary

Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 104 of 2025 establishes enforcement regulations for the Act on Measures to Prevent Sexual Violence against Children by School Establishers and Private Education and Childcare Providers. The listing identifies it as amending the Standards for Equipment and Operation of Child Welfare Facilities (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 63 of 1948), with promulgation on 2025-12-25 and an effective date of 2026-12-25.

Business Impact

Child welfare facility operators may be affected by changes to their equipment and operating standards. Although the amending regulations concern school establishers and private education and childcare providers, this listing specifically identifies an amendment to child welfare facility standards; the substantive changes are not provided.
