Summary

Cabinet Office and Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 13 of 2026 establishes standardization criteria for functions and other requirements of systems processing the administrative tasks listed in Article 9 of the order specifying tasks subject to local government information-system standardization. The metadata records both its promulgation date and effective date as 2026-07-27.

Business Impact

Local governments processing the specified Article 9 tasks may need to consider these standards for the systems used to perform those tasks; the metadata does not identify the individual tasks or detailed system requirements.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese ordinance could clarify the covered Article 9 tasks, required system functions, and any transitional provisions.
