Summary

This item refers to an amending act that revises the Public Offices Election Act (Act No. 100 of 1950) and the Act on measures against rights infringement arising from the distribution of information via specified telecommunications. According to the metadata, the amending act is identified as Act No. 58 of 2026, with a promulgation date and an enforcement date both listed as 2026-07-17. The entry appears in the e-Gov Law Search law update list, so the specific substantive changes are not detailed in the provided metadata.

Business Impact

Organizations involved in elections or in the distribution and handling of information via telecommunications services may be affected, and could need to reassess obligations relating to rights infringement handling.
