Summary

A proposed revision addressing post-quantum cryptography (PQC) to the cryptographic list referenced for electronic government procurement (CRYPTREC Cryptographic List) was published for public comment on 2026-10-27; the stated deadline is 2026-11-11 23:59 JST.
