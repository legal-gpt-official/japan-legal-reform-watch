Summary

This is a Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) ordinance that partially amends the ministerial ordinances governing the business operations, finance and accounting, and personnel management of the Livestock Improvement Center (an incorporated administrative agency) and the Agriculture and Livestock Industries Corporation (ALIC). According to the metadata, the amending ordinance is MAFF Ordinance No. 57 of 2026, with a promulgation date of 2026-08-05 and a stated enforcement date of 2026-08-07.
