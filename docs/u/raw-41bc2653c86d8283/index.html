摘要

2025年第341号政令规定了配合修订《出入国管理及难民认定法》和《外国人技能实习适当实施及技能实习生保护法》的法律施行而对相关政令进行的调整及过渡措施。本条目涉及该政令对《补助金等相关预算适当执行法施行令》（1955年第255号政令）的修订。所载公布日期为2025-10-01，所载生效日期为2027-04-01。

对企业的影响

补助金预算施行令所涵盖的补助金领取者可能受到此次配套修订的影响；本条目中被修订的法规涉及补助金，而非直接规制外国技能实习生。元数据未指明变更的条款或其实际影响。

Summary

Cabinet Order No. 341 of 2025 provides for adjustments to related Cabinet Orders and transitional measures accompanying implementation of an Act amending the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Act on Proper Technical Intern Training and Protection of Technical Intern Trainees. This entry concerns its amendment of the Order for Enforcement of the Act on Appropriate Execution of Budget Pertaining to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955). The stated promulgation date is 2025-10-01, and the stated effective date is 2027-04-01.

Business Impact

Recipients of subsidies covered by the subsidy-budget enforcement order may be affected by this consequential amendment; the instrument amended in this entry concerns subsidies rather than directly regulating foreign technical intern trainees.
