摘要

法务省、厚生劳动省2026年第6号省令于2026-09-30公布，生效日期为2026-10-01。该省令修订了《外国人育成就业适当实施及育成就业外国人保护法施行规则》（法务省、厚生劳动省2025年第4号省令），属于配合综合劳动政策推进、就业稳定及职业生活充实等相关法律修订实施而进行的调整。

Summary

Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 6 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with an effective date of 2026-10-01. It amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on Proper Implementation of Employment for Skill Development of Foreign Nationals and Protection of Foreign Nationals in Employment for Skill Development (Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 4 of 2025), as part of adjustments accompanying implementation of amendments to legislation on comprehensive labor policy, employment stability and working life.

Business Impact

Employers participating in employment for skill development of foreign nationals and foreign nationals in that framework may be affected by changes to its implementing regulations, rather than by general employment rules alone.
