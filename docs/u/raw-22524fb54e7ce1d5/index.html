This is a public comment (opinion solicitation) on a draft ministerial ordinance that would partially amend rules concerning the High Pressure Gas Production Safety Manager examination (under the High Pressure Gas Safety Act) and the enforcement regulations of the Act on Securing Safety and Optimizing Transactions of Liquefied Petroleum Gas.
