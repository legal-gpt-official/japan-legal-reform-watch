Summary

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries published a draft Notice on 2026-07-31 for public comment to amend Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Notice No. 576 of 2018. The existing Notice specifies designated cases under Article 16 of the Act on the Conservation and Sustainable Use of Biological Diversity through Regulations on the Use of Living Modified Organisms for inspection-target organisms whose production or distribution falls under the Minister's jurisdiction. The stated comment deadline is 2026/08/29 at 23:59; the contact is the Agricultural Products Safety Management Division of the Food Safety and Consumer Affairs Bureau, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Business Impact

Producers or distributors of inspection-target genetically modified organisms under the Minister's jurisdiction may be affected by changes to the designated cases, but the specific proposed changes are not described in the available information.

Recommended Action

Reviewing the official Japanese draft and supporting materials would help identify the proposed changes to the designated cases and their scope.
