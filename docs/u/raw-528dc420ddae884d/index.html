This is a draft bill (member-sponsored bill) to partially amend Japan's Act on the Development of an Environment where Young People Can Use the Internet Safely and Securely, listed in the House of Representatives bill records. According to the metadata, it was submitted during the 221st Diet session (bill number 19) and its deliberation status is marked as not concluded.
