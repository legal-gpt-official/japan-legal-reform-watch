Summary

This is a Cabinet-submitted bill (kakuho) concerning special provisions allowing dual employment (kengyo) for national and local public employees in order to facilitate the smooth performance of duties by reserve Self-Defense personnel and related persons. According to the metadata from the House of Representatives legislative information, the deliberation status is marked as 'passed/established' (seiritsu), submitted during Diet session 221, item number 50. This English text is an unofficial summary; the Japanese source is authoritative.
