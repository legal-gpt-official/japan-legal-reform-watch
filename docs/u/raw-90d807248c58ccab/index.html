摘要

2025年第142号政令配合修订《大学等修学支援法》的法律施行，对相关政令作出调整。所列修订涉及《补助金等相关预算执行适正化法施行令》（1955年第255号政令）；所载公布日期为2025-03-31，生效日期为2025-04-01。

对企业的影响

该预算执行施行令涵盖的补助金领取方可能受到此次配套修订的影响。虽然标题涉及高等教育修学支援，但所列被修订法规涉及补助金预算执行，且未提供具体变更内容。

建议采取的措施

Summary

Cabinet Order No. 142 of 2025 adjusts related Cabinet Orders following implementation of an Act amending the Act on Support for Study at Universities and Other Institutions. The listed amendment concerns the Enforcement Order of the Act on Ensuring Proper Execution of Budgets Relating to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955); the stated promulgation date is 2025-03-31 and effective date is 2025-04-01.

Business Impact

Recipients of subsidies covered by the budget-execution enforcement order may be affected by this consequential amendment. Although the headline concerns higher education study support, the listed amended instrument concerns subsidy budget execution, and the specific changes are not provided.
