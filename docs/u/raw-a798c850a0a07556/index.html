Summary

On 2026-11-04, the Digital Agency updated the summary and detailed reports of its FY2025 research on education data linkage. The project piloted fully digital high-school entrance examination administration with the cooperation of the Shizuoka Prefectural Board of Education, building on FY2024 research. It also convened public- and private-sector discussions on cross-regional data linkage and education data standardization. The pilot report identifies implementation considerations for local governments: checking compatibility with existing laws, ordinances and rules; coordinating security policies and rollout arrangements with municipalities; and providing user support and contingency measures. This is a research-report update, not an announcement of new legal obligations.

Business Impact

Local governments, boards of education and school staff may find the pilot findings relevant to digitizing high-school entrance examination procedures, including system selection, municipal coordination and support for students and parents. Education-system vendors may also find the model-specification and existing-service considerations relevant to system procurement and implementation.

Recommended Action

Local governments and boards of education considering digitization could review the report's model-specification guidance, compatibility checks for existing rules and municipal security policies, and arrangements for exceptions and system failures.
