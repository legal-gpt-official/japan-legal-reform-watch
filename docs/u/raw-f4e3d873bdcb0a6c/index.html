The Japan Securities Dealers Association (JSDA) has published the results of a public comment procedure concerning partial amendments to its 'Rules on Equity-Type (Investment-Based) Crowdfunding Business' and related provisions. According to the metadata, the public comment period ran from May 20, 2025 to June 18, 2025. Firms engaged in equity-type crowdfunding business under JSDA self-regulatory rules may be affected by the amended provisions and could need to review their internal procedures.
