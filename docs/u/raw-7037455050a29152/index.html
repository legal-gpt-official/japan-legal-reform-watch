Summary

The Financial Services Agency (FSA) announced a public comment procedure on a draft amendment to the Cabinet Office Order concerning financial instruments business, and on a draft amendment to the standards used to assess whether the capital adequacy of Special Financial Instruments Business Operators and their subsidiaries is appropriate in light of their assets. Both items are described as drafts open for public comment, not finalized rules. The official Japanese source on the FSA website is authoritative.

Business Impact

Special Financial Instruments Business Operators and their subsidiaries could be affected if these draft amendments to the relevant Cabinet Office Order and capital adequacy standards are adopted.
