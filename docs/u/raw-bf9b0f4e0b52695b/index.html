摘要

这是一项政令（令和7年政令第341号，2025-10-01公布），对相关下位政令进行配套调整，并就涉及《出入国管理及难民认定法》及《外国人技能实习法》修正法的生效所需的过渡措施作出规定。e-Gov更新条目中列出的被修正政令为《关于法务大臣就国家利益有关诉讼之权限法》第7条第1款所定公益法人的政令（昭和37年政令第393号）。列明的生效日期为2027-04-01。

对企业的影响

所列修正内容似为对涉及诉讼事务公益法人的政令所作的技术性配套调整，因此对雇主或外国人劳动项目的直接影响可能有限。不过，相关修正法整体框架涉及外国人技能实习及出入境管理规则，对从事相关领域业务的机构可能具有相关性。

Summary

This is a Cabinet Order (Reiwa 7 Cabinet Order No. 341, promulgated 2025-10-01) that makes related adjustments to subordinate orders and sets transitional measures accompanying the entry into force of an amending act covering the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Technical Intern Training Act. The e-Gov update entry lists, as the order being amended, the Cabinet Order designating public corporations under Article 7, Paragraph 1 of the Act on the Authority of the Minister of Justice in Litigation Concerning State Interests (Showa 37 Cabinet Order No. 393). The listed effective date is 2027-04-01.

Business Impact

The listed amendment appears to be a technical conforming change to a Cabinet Order on public corporations in litigation matters, so its direct effect on employers or foreign-worker programs may be limited. However, the broader amending framework concerns foreign technical intern training and immigration rules, which could be relevant to organizations involved in those areas.
