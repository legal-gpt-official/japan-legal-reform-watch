Summary

This is a National Tax Agency (NTA) interpretive circular (law interpretation notice) concerning a partial amendment to the notice on industry categories and per-category stock prices used in the comparable-industry (ruiji gyoushu hijun) valuation method for FY2025 (Reiwa 7). It appears in the NTA's new-information and circulars listing and relates to figures used in property/share valuation. Further details are not provided in the metadata.

Business Impact

Businesses or advisors that value unlisted shares using the comparable-industry method may need to reflect updated industry categories and stock price figures. It could affect valuation calculations for relevant tax purposes.
