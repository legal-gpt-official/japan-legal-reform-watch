Background & Purpose

The Cabinet Legislation Bureau explains that international conditions have increased the importance of secure energy and metal mineral supplies. The government proposes to strengthen the support available for Japanese companies to secure those resources.

The bill would expand JOGMEC's functions to include investment relating to oil transportation and grants for funding carbon dioxide capture, transport and storage. These are proposed public support functions; they do not themselves impose new obligations on private project sponsors.
