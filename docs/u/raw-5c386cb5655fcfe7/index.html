Summary

Cabinet Order No. 196 of 2026 makes amendments to related Cabinet Orders in connection with partial implementation of an Act amending the Industrial Safety and Health Act and the Working Environment Measurement Act. This listing concerns its amendment of the Enforcement Order of the Act on Improvement of Pollution Prevention Organizations in Specified Factories (Cabinet Order No. 264 of 1971), with promulgation on 2026-06-17 and an effective date of 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

Operators of specified factories covered by the pollution prevention organization framework may be affected by this consequential amendment, rather than solely by the occupational safety reforms named in the headline; the listing does not describe the substantive changes.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese source could clarify the amended provisions affecting pollution prevention organizations in specified factories and any transitional arrangements for 2026-10-01.
