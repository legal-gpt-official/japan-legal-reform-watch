Summary

This item is an amending ministerial order from Japan's Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries (MAFF) that partially revises two existing ministerial orders concerning the business operations, finance, accounting, and personnel management of the Japan International Research Center for Agricultural Sciences (JIRCAS) and the National Agriculture and Food Research Organization (NARO). According to the metadata, the amending order (Reiwa 8 MAFF Ministerial Order No. 61) is listed with a promulgation date of 2026-08-05 and a stated effective date of 2026-08-07. The original order is cited as Heisei 13 MAFF Ministerial Order No. 47.

Business Impact

The changes may affect internal governance, financial, accounting, and personnel arrangements at these national research and development agencies, and could be relevant to organizations working with or funded through them.
