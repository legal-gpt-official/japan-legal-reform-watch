Summary

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) has issued a press announcement soliciting organizations to participate in field trials aimed at efficiently developing pedestrian-space network data. According to the announcement, the trials will include technical verification of automatically extracting barrier (accessibility) information using AI. This is a call for participation in a demonstration project rather than a regulatory measure.

Business Impact

Organizations involved in mapping, accessibility data, or AI-based information extraction may find opportunities to participate, and the results could inform future approaches to pedestrian-space data. It could also be relevant to those tracking developments in accessibility and geospatial data initiatives.
