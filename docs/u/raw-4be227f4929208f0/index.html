Summary

According to e-Gov Law Search metadata, this is a National Public Safety Commission rule (Reiwa 8 NPSC Rule No. 17) that reorganizes related NPSC rules in connection with the partial enforcement of a law amending the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act and the Payment Services Act. The metadata lists a promulgation date of 2026-08-10 and an enforcement date of 2026-08-12, and indicates the affected rule includes the enforcement regulation for the law on proper operation of the driver-agency (daiko) service business (Heisei 14 NPSC Rule No. 11). This appears to be a technical, conforming amendment rather than a substantive new regulation, but details should be verified against the official source.

Business Impact

Entities subject to the affected National Public Safety Commission rules may need to note conforming changes, though the impact could be largely technical. Any connection to financial instruments or payment services obligations could warrant closer review.
