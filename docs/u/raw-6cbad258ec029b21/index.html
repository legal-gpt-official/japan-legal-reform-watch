Summary

The e-Gov Law Search update list shows an Act (Reiwa 8 Act No. 40) concerning a special exception for national and local public servants' side-work (concurrent employment) to facilitate the smooth performance of duties by reserve Self-Defense Forces personnel and similar roles. The metadata lists a promulgation date of 2026-06-17 and an enforcement date of 2027-04-01. The summary relies only on the title and basic metadata, not the statutory text.

Business Impact

This may mainly concern national and local government employers and public servants who also serve as reserve SDF personnel, and could have limited direct relevance for most private-sector businesses.
