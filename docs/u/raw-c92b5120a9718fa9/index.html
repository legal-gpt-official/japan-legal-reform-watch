Summary

Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI), Manufacturing Industries Bureau, Industrial Machinery Division, has opened a public comment on a draft policy for ensuring the stable supply of machine tools and industrial robots. The draft is issued under the Act on the Promotion of Ensuring National Security through Integrated Implementation of Economic Measures (Economic Security Promotion Act). According to the metadata, the draft was published on 2026-09-01 with a comment deadline of 2026-09-30 at 17:00.
