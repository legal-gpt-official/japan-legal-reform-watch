Summary

Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 87 of 2026 amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Maternal and Child Health Act (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 55 of 1965). It adjusts Cabinet Office ordinances related to the Children and Families Agency following partial implementation of the Act Partially Amending the Medical Care Act and Other Acts. The stated promulgation date is 2026-09-30, and the stated effective date is 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

Those subject to the maternal and child health regulations may be affected by these amendments, rather than medical care providers generally; the available information does not identify the changed requirements or specific affected categories.

Recommended Action

Reviewing the official Japanese ordinance could clarify which maternal and child health provisions changed and whether any transitional provisions apply.
