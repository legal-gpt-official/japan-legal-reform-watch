Summary

This item is a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) ministerial ordinance that amends related ministerial rules in connection with the partial enforcement of a law amending the Industrial Safety and Health Act and the Working Environment Measurement Act. According to the metadata, it targets the ministerial ordinance on registration and designation under the Industrial Safety and Health Act and related orders (Ministry of Labour Ordinance No. 44 of 1972), and is identified as MHLW Ordinance No. 116 of 2026. The metadata lists a promulgation date of 2026-07-29 and a stated effective date of 2026-09-01.

Business Impact

Organizations subject to registration or designation requirements under the Industrial Safety and Health Act and related working environment measurement rules may be affected by the amended ministerial provisions.
