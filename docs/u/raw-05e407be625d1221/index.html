This e-Gov law update list entry shows a cabinet order (Reiwa 8 Cabinet Order No. 274) with a listed promulgation date of 2026-09-11 and an effective date of 2026-10-01. According to the metadata, it amends the Enforcement Order of the Act on Anonymized and Pseudonymized Medical Information for Medical R&D (Heisei 30 Cabinet Order No. 163) as part of arrangements accompanying the partial enforcement of an amending act to the Medical Care Act and related laws.
