Summary

This item concerns an amending law (Reiwa 8 Law No. 58) that partially revises the Public Offices Election Act and the Act on the Handling of Rights Infringements and Other Matters Arising from the Distribution of Information via Specified Telecommunications (Heisei 13 Law No. 137). According to the metadata, the source lists a promulgation date and an enforcement date both of 2026-07-17. This entry appears in an e-Gov law update list.

Business Impact

Organizations involved in telecommunications, online platforms, or handling of information related to rights infringements and election matters could be affected by changes to the underlying statutes.
