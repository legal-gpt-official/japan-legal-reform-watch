Summary

The National Tax Agency (NTA) announced on 2026-03-31 a Notice No. 5 amending National Tax Agency Notice No. 5 of 2018. The amended notice specifies attachment documents and related materials designated by the NTA Commissioner under Article 5, paragraph 3, item 4 of the Ministerial Ordinance on promoting the use of information and communications technology in administration under national tax laws.
