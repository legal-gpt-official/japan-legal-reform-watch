摘要

2026年厚生劳动省令第142号于2026-09-30公布，载明的生效日期为2027-05-20。该省令配合《药品、医疗器械等质量、有效性及安全性确保等法等部分修正法》的部分施行，对《药品、医药部外品、化妆品及再生医疗等产品质量管理标准省令》（2004年厚生劳动省令第136号）作出修订。

对企业的影响

适用药品、医药部外品、化妆品及再生医疗等产品质量管理标准的主体可能受到这些标准变更的影响。虽然修订省令的标题也提及医疗器械，但所指明的修订对象省令涉及上述四类产品。

建议采取的措施

可考虑查阅日文官方省令，以明确修订后的质量管理条款、任何过渡安排及其自2027-05-20起的适用情况。

Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 142 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with a stated effective date of 2027-05-20. It amends the Ministerial Ordinance on Standards for Quality Control of Pharmaceuticals, Quasi-drugs, Cosmetics and Regenerative Medical Products (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 136 of 2004), in connection with partial implementation of an Act amending the Act on Securing Quality, Efficacy and Safety of Products Including Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices and other laws.

Business Impact

Those subject to quality-control standards for pharmaceuticals, quasi-drugs, cosmetics and regenerative medical products may be affected by changes to those standards. Although the amending ordinance's title also references medical devices, the identified target ordinance concerns the four specified product categories.

Recommended Action

Reviewing the official Japanese ordinance could clarify the amended quality-control provisions, any transitional arrangements and their application from 2027-05-20.
