Summary

This item concerns a Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) ministerial ordinance that partially amends rules governing the business operations, finance, accounting, and personnel management of METI-affiliated research institutes, including the National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology (AIST). According to the metadata, the amending ordinance (Reiwa 8 METI Ordinance No. 68) lists a promulgation date and an enforcement date of 2026-08-07. The affected base ordinance is identified as Heisei 13 METI Ordinance No. 108.
