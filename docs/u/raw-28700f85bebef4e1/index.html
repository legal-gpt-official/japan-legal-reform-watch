Summary

This item concerns a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) ministerial ordinance related to amendments and transitional measures for MHLW-related ministerial ordinances, tied to a law amending the National Pension Act and others to strengthen the financial base and minimum guarantee function of the public pension system. According to the metadata, it affects the Enforcement Regulations of the National Pension Act (Ordinance No. 12 of 1960), with the amending ordinance being MHLW Ordinance No. 11 of 2017.
