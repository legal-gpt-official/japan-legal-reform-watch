Summary

This is a public comment invitation on a draft ministerial ordinance that would partially amend the enforcement regulations concerning payment of national revenue and similar amounts using information and communication technology, in relation to laws under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT). According to the metadata, the draft was announced on 2026/07/01 with a comment submission deadline of 2026/08/01 00:00. The item is categorized under promotion of IT-enabled society, and inquiries are directed to the MLIT Minister's Secretariat Accounts Division.

Business Impact

Organizations that make electronic payments of national revenue in connection with MLIT-related procedures could be affected if the proposed amendments are adopted.
