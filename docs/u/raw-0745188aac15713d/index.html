摘要

2019年第17号政令规定了配合《儿童及育儿支援法》修正法实施的相关政令调整及过渡措施。所列被修订法令为《补助金等预算执行适正化法施行令》（1955年第255号政令）。所载公布日期为2019-05-31，生效日期为2019-10-01。

Summary

Cabinet Order No. 17 of 2019 provides for adjustments to related Cabinet Orders and transitional measures accompanying implementation of an Act amending the Child and Child Care Support Act. The listed instrument amended is the Order for Enforcement of the Act on Regulation of Execution of Budget Pertaining to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955). The stated promulgation date is 2019-05-31 and the effective date is 2019-10-01.

Business Impact
