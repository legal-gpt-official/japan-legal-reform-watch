Summary

This e-Gov Law Search update entry lists a Cabinet Order (Reiwa 7 Cabinet Order No. 341, promulgated 2025-10-01) that arranges related ordinances and transitional measures accompanying the enforcement of an amending act to the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Technical Intern Training Act. The entry shows the order amending the Cabinet Order for the Act on Promotion of Administration Using Information and Communications Technology (Heisei 15 Cabinet Order No. 27), with a listed enforcement date of 2027-04-01.
