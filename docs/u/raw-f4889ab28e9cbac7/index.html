Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 142 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with an effective date of 2027-05-20. It amends the Clinical Research Act Enforcement Regulations (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 17 of 2018) as part of adjustments to ministry ordinances accompanying partial implementation of an Act amending the Act on Securing Quality, Efficacy and Safety of Products Including Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices and other laws.

Business Impact

Those conducting clinical research may be affected by changes to the Clinical Research Act Enforcement Regulations; the listed amendment concerns clinical research specifically, rather than pharmaceutical and medical-device activities generally. The metadata does not identify the provisions changed or their operational effects.
