Summary

Cabinet Order No. 55 of 2018 provides for adjustments to related Cabinet Orders and transitional measures accompanying implementation of the Act partially amending the Long-Term Care Insurance Act and other laws to strengthen community-based integrated care systems. The listed amendment concerns the Enforcement Order of the Act on the Appropriateness of Budget Execution for Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955); the stated promulgation date is 2018-03-22 and the effective date is 2018-04-01.

Business Impact

Recipients of subsidies covered by the budget-execution enforcement order may be affected by this consequential amendment. The listed target concerns subsidy administration rather than long-term care insurance directly, but the specific changes and affected recipients are not described.

Recommended Action

Reviewing the official Japanese source would help clarify the amendments to the subsidy budget-execution enforcement order and any applicable transitional provisions.
