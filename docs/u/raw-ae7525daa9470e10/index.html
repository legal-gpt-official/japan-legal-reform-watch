Summary

Cabinet Secretariat Ordinance No. 10 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-10-22, with an effective date of 2026-11-02. It amends the ordinance prescribing document forms for recruiting and approving early-retirement applicants under the National Public Employees Retirement Allowance Act (Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications Ordinance No. 58 of 2013). It also amends the Cabinet Secretariat ordinance on special rules for basic retirement allowance amounts under paragraphs 12, 14 and 16 of that Act's supplementary provisions.
