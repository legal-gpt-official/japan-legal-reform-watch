Summary

This is an unofficial summary of a Japanese Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) ordinance that amends related MHLW ordinances in connection with the partial enforcement of a law amending the Act on Securing Quality, Efficacy and Safety of Pharmaceuticals, Medical Devices, and Other Products (PMD Act) and related laws. According to the metadata, the amending ordinance (Reiwa 8 MHLW Ordinance No. 110) affects an ordinance on the use of information and communication technology in the retention of documents by private business operators under MHLW-administered laws (Heisei 17 MHLW Ordinance No. 44). The metadata lists a promulgation date of 2026-06-30 and an enforcement date of 2027-05-20.

Business Impact

Businesses subject to MHLW recordkeeping and electronic document-retention rules in the pharmaceuticals and medical device sector may need to review how these amendments affect their document handling obligations.
