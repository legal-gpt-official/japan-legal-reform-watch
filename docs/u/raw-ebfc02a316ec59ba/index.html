Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 140 of 2026 amends the ministry's ordinance on certification and related matters for supply assurance plans under the Act on the Promotion of Ensuring National Security through Integrated Implementation of Economic Measures (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 8 of 2023).
