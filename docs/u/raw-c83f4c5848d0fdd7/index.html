Summary

Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) issued a government announcement under Article 34, paragraphs 3 and 4 of the Act on Special Measures Concerning Renewable Energy. According to the metadata, the Organization for Cross-regional Coordination of Transmission Operators notified METI that an electricity business operator had not remitted the required levy (surcharges paid by electricity users) by the deadline set in a reminder notice (June 10, 2026), and METI is therefore publicly naming that operator. The operator's name is not included in the provided metadata.

Business Impact

Electricity business operators may wish to note that failure to remit the levy after a reminder could lead to public naming by METI, which could carry reputational consequences. The metadata does not indicate any new obligations for other businesses.

Recommended Action

Review the official METI release in Japanese to confirm the named operator, the details of the announcement, and the underlying statutory provisions.
