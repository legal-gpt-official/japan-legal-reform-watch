Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 28 of 2026 makes amendments associated with partial implementation of the Act Partially Amending the Medical Care Act and Other Acts. The listed target is the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on Promotion of Comprehensive Securing of Medical and Long-Term Care in Communities (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 34 of 1989). The amending ordinance was promulgated on 2026-03-19, with the listed amendment effective on 2027-04-01.
