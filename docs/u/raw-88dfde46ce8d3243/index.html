Summary

The Ministry of the Environment has opened a public comment period on a draft proposal to review target values and related items for agricultural chemicals (pesticides) in drinking water. According to the metadata, the draft was announced on 2026/09/24 and comments are accepted until 2026/10/24. The matter is handled by the Water Supply Quality and Sanitation Management Office of the Ministry of the Environment's Water and Atmospheric Environment Bureau.

Business Impact

Organizations involved in drinking water supply, water quality testing, or agricultural chemical use may wish to monitor this draft, as any resulting changes could affect water quality standards or monitoring targets.
