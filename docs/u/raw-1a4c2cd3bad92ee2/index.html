Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 142 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with a stated effective date of 2027-05-20, to adjust ministry ordinances following partial implementation of an Act amending legislation on the quality, efficacy and safety of pharmaceuticals and medical devices. The listed amendment concerns the Ordinance on the Use of Information and Communications Technology in Document Preservation by Private Businesses and Others under Laws Administered by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 44 of 2005).

Business Impact

Private businesses and others preserving documents under laws administered by the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare may be affected through changes to electronic document storage rules. The listed target is this document-preservation ordinance, rather than pharmaceutical or medical-device requirements generally; the specific changes are not provided.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese amendment for the affected electronic document-preservation provisions, their scope and the stated 2027-05-20 effective date.
