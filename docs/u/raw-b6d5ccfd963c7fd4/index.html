Summary

A public consultation concerns the proposed lifting of designated former supply-point designations for deemed gas retailers formerly operating simplified gas businesses, within the Kanto Bureau of Economy, Trade and Industry's jurisdiction. The proposal was announced on 2026/06/15, with comments accepted until 2026/07/15 23:59; the contact office is the bureau's Gas Business Division, Resources, Energy and Environment Department.

Business Impact

Deemed gas retailers formerly operating simplified gas businesses may be affected because the proposal concerns the designations of their former supply points under the Kanto bureau's jurisdiction.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese consultation materials could clarify which retailers and supply points are covered, the effects of lifting the designations, and the comment submission requirements.
