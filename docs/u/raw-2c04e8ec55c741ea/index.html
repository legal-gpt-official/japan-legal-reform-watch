Summary

This item concerns a Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW) ministerial ordinance amending the Enforcement Regulations under Japan's Preventive Vaccination Law (originally MHW Ordinance No. 27 of 1958). According to the metadata, the amending ordinance is MHLW Ordinance No. 84 of 2023, with a stated promulgation date and enforcement date of June 19, 2023. It is linked to amendments related to the law on prevention of infectious diseases and medical care for infectious disease patients, and appears in an e-Gov Law Search update list.
