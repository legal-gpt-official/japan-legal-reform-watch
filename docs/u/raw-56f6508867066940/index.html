Summary

Cabinet Order No. 154 of 2021 sets the amount specified by Cabinet Order under Article 16(1) of the Act on Registration of Deposit and Savings Accounts for the Prompt and Reliable Provision of Public Benefits. The listing identifies it as amending the Enforcement Order of the Act on Regulation of Execution of Budget Pertaining to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955), with promulgation and effective dates of 2021-05-19.

Recipients of subsidies covered by the amended enforcement order may be affected; the identified amendment concerns subsidy budget execution rather than solely public-benefit account registration. The metadata does not specify the amount or the provisions changed.
