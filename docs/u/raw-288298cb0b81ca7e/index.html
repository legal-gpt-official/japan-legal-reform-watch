This is a press release from Japan's Ministry of the Environment (MOE) regarding the promulgation of amendments to two notices: one on the general and industrial waste subject to special provisions for detoxification (harmless) processing, and one on the standards for the content of detoxification treatment relating to low-concentration polychlorinated biphenyl (PCB) waste.
