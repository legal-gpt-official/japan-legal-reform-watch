Summary

This item concerns a Cabinet Order arranging related Cabinet Orders in connection with the enforcement of a law partially amending the Volunteer Probation Officer Act and related statutes to enhance the rehabilitation and protection (offender rehabilitation) system. According to the metadata, it is Cabinet Order No. 253 of 2026, with a promulgation date of 2026-08-07 and an enforcement date of 2026-12-01.
