Summary

This item concerns a Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries ministerial order that partially amends the ministerial orders on the operations, finance, accounting, and personnel management of the Japan International Research Center for Agricultural Sciences (JIRCAS) and on the finance, accounting, and personnel management related to the agricultural and food-industry technology research and agricultural mechanization promotion activities of the National Agriculture and Food Research Organization (NARO). According to the metadata, the amending order (Reiwa 8 MAFF Ordinance No. 61) carries a promulgation date of 2026-08-05 and a stated enforcement date of 2026-08-07.
