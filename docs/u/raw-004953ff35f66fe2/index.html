Summary

This item, from the e-Gov Law Search law-update list, concerns an amending law described as 'Act to Partially Amend the Industrial Competitiveness Enhancement Act and Related Laws to Promote the Sustainable Development of Corporate Business Activities in Light of Changes in Economic and Social Conditions' (Reiwa 8 Act No. 29). According to the metadata, it amends a related statute on the sustainable supply and fair transactions of food products (Heisei 3 Act No. 59), with a promulgation date of 2026-06-05 and an indicated enforcement date of 2026-07-31. The details are drawn only from the update-list metadata and should be verified against the official Japanese source.
