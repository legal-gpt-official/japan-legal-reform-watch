Summary

On 2026-10-23, the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications announced a public-comment consultation on a draft Notice partially amending Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications Notice No. 43 of 2026. The existing Notice specifies frequency ranges and related matters for specified experimental radio stations under Article 7, item 5 of the Regulations for Enforcement of the Radio Act; the title does not specify the proposed changes or a comment deadline.

Business Impact

Operators of specified experimental radio stations may be affected because the draft concerns the frequency ranges available for those stations.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese consultation materials could clarify the proposed frequency changes, their scope and the comment deadline.
