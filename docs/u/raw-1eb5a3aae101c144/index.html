摘要

2026年第305号政令就《防灾厅设置法》及配套的《关于伴随防灾厅设置法施行而整备相关法律等的法律》的实施作出相应修订。所列修订涉及1999年第253号政令，该政令规定了《重要影响事态下确保日本和平及安全措施法》第3条第1款第4项所指的相关行政机关。所载公布日期为2026-09-30，所载生效日期为2026-11-02。

对企业的影响

该修订可能影响参与重要影响事态下确保日本和平及安全措施的行政机关的指定，而非一般防灾业务。该列表并未确定其对私营企业有任何直接影响。

Summary

Cabinet Order No. 305 of 2026 makes consequential amendments associated with implementation of the Disaster Prevention Agency Establishment Act and the accompanying act adjusting related laws. The listed amendment concerns Cabinet Order No. 253 of 1999, which specifies the relevant administrative organs under Article 3, paragraph 1, item 4 of the Act on Measures to Ensure Japan's Peace and Security in Situations Having an Important Influence. The stated promulgation date is 2026-09-30, and the stated effective date is 2026-11-02.

Business Impact

The amendment may affect the designation of administrative organs involved in measures to ensure Japan's peace and security in situations having an important influence, rather than disaster-management businesses generally. The listing does not establish any direct effect on private businesses.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese text to check which administrative organs are changed in Cabinet Order No. 253 of 1999 and any transitional provisions associated with the stated effective date.
