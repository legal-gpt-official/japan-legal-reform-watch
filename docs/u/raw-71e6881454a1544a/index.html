Summary

Joint Ministerial Ordinance No. 3 of 2026 amends Joint Ministerial Ordinance No. 2 of 2017 concerning approved collaborative support projects under Article 33 of the Act on Strengthening the Foundations for Regional Growth and Development through the Promotion of Regional Economy-Driving Projects. Both ordinances are issued jointly by the ministries of Internal Affairs and Communications; Finance; Health, Labour and Welfare; Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries; Economy, Trade and Industry; Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism; and the Environment.
