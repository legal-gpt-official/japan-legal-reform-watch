Summary

The National Tax Agency (NTA) published an item concerning a partial amendment to its notice on industry categories and per-category stock prices used for the comparable-industry (ruiji gyoushu hijun) valuation method for the year Reiwa 8 (2026). It is presented as a law-interpretation circular (hourei kaishaku tsuutatsu). Details beyond the title and source metadata are not provided here.

Business Impact

This may affect how certain unlisted-share valuations are calculated for tax purposes for the relevant period, potentially influencing valuation figures for affected businesses.
