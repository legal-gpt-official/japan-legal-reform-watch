Summary

This item is a Cabinet Office Ordinance that partially amends provisions concerning the format of identification certificates carried by officials during on-site inspections under the Enforcement Regulations of the Child Welfare Act and other laws administered by the Children and Families Agency. According to the provided metadata, the amending ordinance is Reiwa 8 Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 75, with a stated promulgation date and enforcement date of 2026-09-07. The underlying regulation is the Enforcement Regulations of the Child Welfare Act (Showa 23 Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 11).

Business Impact
