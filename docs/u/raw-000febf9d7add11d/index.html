摘要

2026-11-06，数字厅宣布发布关于政府AI“源内”中行政实务用检索增强生成（RAG）应用的讲解视频；这是一则信息公告，并非监管修订。这些应用参考已登记的手册、指南和报告来回答问题，并整理、分析、比较或汇总信息。示例包括EASY电子行政文书管理系统问答应用（可能并非所有府省厅均可使用），以及供数字厅内部使用的文书管理问答应用。讲解提醒，AI回答可能包含错误或遗漏，并呼吁核查原始来源，尤其是日期、文书编号和数值，同时遵守各府省厅关于允许在“源内”中使用的信息的规则。数字厅还介绍了根据请求开发预计可供多个府省厅使用的应用的做法，完成的应用通常会在“源内”上发布。

对企业的影响

使用“源内”的府省厅可能能够减少查找行政手册和处理内部咨询所花费的时间。应用的可用性及允许输入的信息可能因府省厅而异；该公告并未为私营企业设立新的义务。

建议采取的措施

潜在的政府用户可考虑在使用前查看应用的可用性及其所属府省厅关于允许使用的信息的规则，并将引用的文件和页面与AI生成的回答进行对照。考虑提出开发请求的府省厅可考虑核查其拟议应用是否适合跨多个府省厅使用，并确定需提供的参考文件。

Summary

On 2026-11-06, the Digital Agency announced an explanatory video on administrative retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) apps in Government AI Gennai; this is an informational announcement, not a regulatory amendment. The apps reference registered manuals, guidelines and reports to answer questions and organize, analyze, compare or summarize information. Examples include an EASY electronic administrative document management system Q&A app, which may not be available in every ministry or agency, and a document-management Q&A app for internal Digital Agency use. The explanation warns that AI answers may contain errors or omissions and calls for checking original sources, particularly dates, document numbers and numerical values, and observing each ministry or agency's rules on information permitted in Gennai. The Agency also describes development on request for apps expected to be used across multiple ministries or agencies, with completed apps generally published on Gennai.

Business Impact

Ministries and agencies using Gennai may be able to reduce time spent searching administrative manuals and handling internal inquiries. Availability and permitted information inputs may differ by ministry or agency; the announcement does not establish a new obligation for private businesses.

Recommended Action
