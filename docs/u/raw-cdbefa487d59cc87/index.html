Summary

Japan's Financial Services Agency has opened a public comment period on a draft Cabinet Office Order amending the order concerning Financial Instruments Business, and a related draft amendment to the criteria for assessing the capital adequacy of Special Financial Instruments Business Operators and their subsidiaries. According to the metadata, the drafts were announced on 2026/07/31 with a comment deadline of 2026/08/31 17:00, under the categories of finance and insurance. This is a draft out for public comment, not an enacted rule.

Business Impact

Special Financial Instruments Business Operators and their subsidiaries may be affected if the proposed capital-adequacy assessment criteria and related requirements are ultimately adopted.
