摘要

数字厅提议增加一种身份验证方式，使用智能手机中存储的、可替代个人编号卡并提供同等功能的电子记录。该厅令草案拟部分修订《为迅速且确实实施公共给付发放等事项而进行存款储蓄账户登记等事项的法律施行规则》。数字厅于2026-11-12公布该草案并公开征求意见，注明的征求意见期间为2026-11-12至2026-12-11；意见须以日语通过e-Gov意见表提交。摘录未明确生效日期或其他拟议修订的完整范围。

对企业的影响

如果提案获采纳，登记用于接收公共给付的存款或储蓄账户的人员可能获得一种额外的智能手机身份验证选项。摘录未确立任何新的强制验证要求。

建议采取的措施

可考虑审查草案中使用智能手机进行验证的资格及程序条件，包括其与现有账户登记方式如何衔接。

Summary

The Digital Agency proposed adding an identity-verification method using a smartphone-held electronic record that substitutes for an Individual Number Card and provides equivalent functions. The draft Agency Ordinance would partially amend the Regulation for Enforcement of the Act on Registration of Deposit and Savings Accounts for the Prompt and Reliable Payment of Public Benefits. The agency published the draft for public comment on 2026-11-12, with a stated consultation period of 2026-11-12 to 2026-12-11; submissions were required to be in Japanese through the e-Gov comment form. The excerpt does not specify an effective date or the full scope of the other proposed amendments.

Business Impact

People registering deposit or savings accounts for public benefit payments may gain an additional smartphone-based identity-verification option if the proposal is adopted. The excerpt does not establish any new mandatory verification requirement.

Recommended Action

A review could check the draft's eligibility and procedural conditions for smartphone-based verification, including how it would interact with existing account-registration methods.
