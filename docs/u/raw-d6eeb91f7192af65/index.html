Summary

This is an order (meirei) establishing standardization criteria for functions and other requirements of systems used to process the tasks specified in each item of Article 12, under the framework of the Act on the Standardization of Local Government Information Systems. According to the provided metadata, the order carries law number Reiwa 8 Cabinet Office / Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Order No. 16, with both a promulgation date and an effective date listed as 2026-08-07. The item was surfaced via an e-Gov law update list; the official Japanese source is authoritative and should be consulted for the actual text.

Business Impact

Organizations involved with local government information systems may need to review whether the specified standardization criteria affect the design or functionality of relevant systems.
