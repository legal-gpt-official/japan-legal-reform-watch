Summary

Japan's Food Safety Commission (under the Cabinet Office) has opened a public comment period on a draft deliberation result concerning the food health impact assessment of 2'-fucosyllactose produced using the Escherichia coli K-12 DH1 MDO MAP1001h strain. According to the metadata, the draft was posted on 2026-09-09, with comments accepted until 2026-10-08. This is a draft assessment out for public comment, not a finalized decision.

Companies producing, importing, or using 2'-fucosyllactose from this microbial source may wish to monitor the outcome, as it could affect how this ingredient is evaluated for food safety in Japan.
