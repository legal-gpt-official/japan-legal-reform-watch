摘要

厚生劳动省2026年第142号省令于2026-09-30公布，载明的生效日期为2027-05-20。该省令修订了《再生医疗等安全性确保法施行规则》（厚生劳动省2014年第110号省令），属于配合《药品、医疗器械等质量、有效性及安全性确保等法等部分修订法》部分施行而对厚生劳动省相关省令进行调整的一部分。

Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 142 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with a stated effective date of 2027-05-20. It amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on the Safety of Regenerative Medicine (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 110 of 2014) as part of adjustments to ministry ordinances accompanying partial implementation of an Act amending legislation on the quality, efficacy and safety of pharmaceuticals and medical devices.
