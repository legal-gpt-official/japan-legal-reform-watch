Summary

The Financial Services Agency (Kinyucho) announced the publication of consultative documents by the BIS Committee on Payments and Market Infrastructures (CPMI) and the International Organization of Securities Commissions (IOSCO). According to the title, these include a consultative document on a cyber resilience practical toolkit with practical considerations for Financial Market Infrastructures (FMIs), and a discussion paper on FMI dependencies on third-party service providers and related challenges and risks. This appears to be a government announcement referring to international consultative/discussion materials.

Business Impact

Firms operating or relying on financial market infrastructures may wish to note these international consultation materials, as they could inform future expectations on cyber resilience and third-party risk management.
