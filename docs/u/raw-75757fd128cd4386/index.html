Summary

Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 77 of 2026 amends Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 17 of 2026, which sets standardization criteria for functions and other requirements of systems processing specified local-government administrative tasks. The listing gives both the promulgation date and the effective date as 2026-09-14; it does not describe the substantive changes.

Business Impact

Local governments processing the specified administrative tasks may be affected by changes to the standardization criteria for their information systems. The metadata does not establish which system functions or requirements have changed.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese amendment to identify the changed standardization criteria, the administrative tasks covered, and any transitional provisions.
