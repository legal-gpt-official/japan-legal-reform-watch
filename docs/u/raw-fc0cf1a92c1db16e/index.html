Summary

Japan's Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries has opened a public comment period on a draft partial amendment to the ministerial ordinance concerning composition standards for feed and feed additives. The draft was announced on 2026-08-28, with comments accepted until 2026-09-26 at 23:59. The item is categorized under agriculture and is handled by the Livestock and Aquaculture Safety Management Division of the Consumer Affairs Bureau.

Business Impact

Businesses involved in the manufacture, import, or distribution of feed and feed additives may be affected if the proposed standards are adopted. The impact remains uncertain while the amendment is still at the draft stage.
