Summary

Japan's Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI), through its Battery Industry Division, has opened a public comment period on a proposed amendment to the policy for ensuring the stable supply of storage batteries, based on the Act on Promotion of Ensuring Security by Integrated Implementation of Economic Measures (Economic Security Promotion Act). According to the metadata, the draft was published on 2026-09-04 and comments are accepted until 2026-10-05. This is a public comment (consultation) on a proposed amendment, not a finalized measure.
