Summary

Japan's Ministry of the Environment (MOE) has issued a public comment (public comment) notice concerning draft amendments to the Cabinet Order and Ministerial Ordinance enforcing the Act on the Prevention of Adverse Ecological Impacts Caused by Designated Invasive Alien Species, as well as a draft revision to the notice setting detailed standards for designated rearing facilities under the Minister of the Environment. The materials are presented as drafts open for public comment and are not described as enacted or in force. The subject area relates to the natural environment.

Business Impact

Organizations that rear, handle, or maintain facilities for designated invasive alien species could be affected if the proposed standards and rules are adopted.
