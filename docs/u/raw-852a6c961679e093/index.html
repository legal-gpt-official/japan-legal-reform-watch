Summary

Act No. 63 of 2023 amends the Basic Act on the Formation of a Digital Society and other laws to promote regulatory reform for a digital society. This listing concerns its amendment to the Long-Term Credit Bank Act (Act No. 187 of 1952), with a promulgation date of 2023-06-16 and a stated effective date of 2024-04-01.
