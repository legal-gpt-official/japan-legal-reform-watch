Summary

A public-comment announcement published on 2026-07-31 covers a draft ministerial ordinance amending the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on Stabilization of Supply, Demand and Prices of Staple Food and related rules, plus two draft enforcement regulations jointly concerning the Ministry of Finance and the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries: one under that Act and one under the Act on the Use of Information and Communications Technology in the Preservation of Documents by Private Business Operators and Others. The stated comment deadline is 2026/08/29 at 23:59; the contact is the Planning Division, Agricultural Policy Department, Crop Production Bureau, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.

Business Impact

Operators subject to staple food supply, demand and price rules, and private business operators and others preserving documents under laws administered by the two ministries, may be affected by the proposed regulatory changes. The listing does not establish the specific requirements or changes to electronic recordkeeping.

Recommended Action

Reviewing the official consultation documents would help clarify the proposed amendments, the scope of the two proposed enforcement regulations, and any implementation dates.
