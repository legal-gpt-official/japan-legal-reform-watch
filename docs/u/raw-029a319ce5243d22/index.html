Summary

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 72 of 2026 amends the enforcement regulations for the Farmland Act and the Act on Promoting Renewable Energy Electricity Generation Harmonized with Sound Development of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries. The update also identifies amendments to the ministry's Enforcement Regulations for the Act on Special Zones for Reconstruction in Response to the Great East Japan Earthquake (Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 66 of 2011). The stated promulgation date is 2026-10-09, and the stated effective date is 2027-04-01.

Business Impact

Farmland users and renewable electricity generation operators in agriculture, forestry and fisheries may be affected by the named regulatory amendments. However, the listed target instrument concerns reconstruction special zones, so the specific effects on activities within those zones cannot be established from the listing alone.

Recommended Action
