Summary

Cabinet Order No. 305 of 2026 makes consequential amendments associated with implementation of the Disaster Management Agency Establishment Act and its related legislation-adjustment Act. The listed amended instrument is the Enforcement Order of the Act Prohibiting Flights of Small Unmanned Aircraft and Other Devices over Areas around Important Facilities (Cabinet Order No. 224 of 2016). The metadata gives both the promulgation date and effective date as 2026-09-30.

Business Impact

Operators of small unmanned aircraft and other devices around important facilities may be affected because the listed consequential amendment concerns the flight-prohibition enforcement order, rather than disaster management generally; the specific changes are not provided.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese source could clarify which provisions of Cabinet Order No. 224 of 2016 change and how they relate to establishment of the Disaster Management Agency.
