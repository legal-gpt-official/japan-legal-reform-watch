Summary

The Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (Soumu-sho) has opened a public comment period on a draft revision to guidelines concerning Article 26 of the law addressing rights infringement arising from information distribution via specified telecommunications. According to the metadata, the proposal was published on 2026-07-03 with a comment deadline of 2026-07-16 23:59, and is categorized under telecommunications. This is a draft out for public comment, not an enacted or finalized measure.
