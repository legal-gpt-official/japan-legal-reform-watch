摘要

该政令草案拟扩大移动通信用户身份确认法第8条第1款第2项所列犯罪的范围，纳入特定的非法访问及犯罪收益相关犯罪，并扩展另一项现有犯罪条目。草案还拟更新犯罪指定政令、公益通报者保护施行政令及《警察厅组织令》中的法律引用，以反映移动通信法律名称中删除“语音”一词的变化。这些修订配合修订《移动语音通信运营商对签约用户等的本人身份确认等及移动语音通信服务不当使用防止法》（2005年第31号法律）的2026年第25号法律的施行。草案规定于2027-04-01生效，该日期为修订法所载明的生效日期。公开征求意见公告发布于2026-11-20，载明的截止时间为2026-12-21 23:59（日本标准时间）；所列主管机关为总务省和警察厅。

建议采取的措施

移动通信运营商可考虑结合相关法律条文审阅拟新增至第8条第1款第2项犯罪清单的内容，包括其与签约用户办理程序的关联，以及拟定的2027-04-01生效日期。

Summary

The draft Cabinet Order would expand the offenses listed under Article 8(1)(ii) of the mobile subscriber identification law to include specified unauthorized-access and criminal-proceeds offenses, and extend another existing offense entry. It would also update statutory references in the offense-designation order, the whistleblower-protection implementing order and the National Police Agency Organization Order, reflecting the removal of 'voice' from the mobile communications law's title. These amendments accompany the implementing Act No. 25 of 2026 amending the Act on Identification of Subscribers by Mobile Voice Communications Carriers and Prevention of Improper Use of Mobile Voice Communications Services (Act No. 31 of 2005). The draft specifies commencement on 2027-04-01, the stated commencement date of the amending Act. The public-comment notice was published on 2026-11-20, with a stated deadline of 2026-12-21 23:59 JST; the listed authorities are the Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications and the National Police Agency.

Business Impact

Mobile communications carriers and their subscribers may be affected by the expanded list of offenses referenced in the subscriber-identification and misuse-prevention framework. Recommended Action

Mobile communications carriers could review the proposed additions to the Article 8(1)(ii) offense list alongside the underlying statutory provision, including their relevance to subscriber-handling procedures and the proposed 2027-04-01 commencement.
