Draft notice amending labeling requirements for specified products containing PFOS or its salts

要約

2026年9月1日、ＰＦＯＳ又はその塩等が使用されている所定の製品について、容器、包装又は送り状に表示すべき環境汚染防止措置等の事項を一部改正する案が公示され、意見公募の対象とされました。受付締切は2026年10月1日00:00 JSTで、問合せ先は環境省大臣官房環境保健部化学物質安全課化学物質審査室です。

事業への影響

一份部分修订含PFOS或其盐及其他涵盖物质的指定产品容器、包装或发货单标示要求的告示草案于2026-09-01发布，公开征求意见。相关要求涉及对《化学物质审查及制造等规制法施行令》附则所指定的产品采取防止第一种特定化学物质造成环境污染的措施；所列意见提交截止时间为2026-10-01 00:00（日本标准时间）。所列联系部门为环境省大臣官房环境保健部化学物质安全课化学物质审查室。

Summary

A draft notice partially amending labeling requirements for containers, packaging or invoices for specified products containing PFOS or its salts and other covered substances was published for public comment on 2026-09-01. The requirements concern measures to prevent environmental pollution by Class I Specified Chemical Substances for products specified under the supplementary provisions of the Enforcement Order of the Act on the Evaluation of Chemical Substances and Regulation of Their Manufacture, etc.; the stated comment deadline is 2026-10-01 00:00 JST. The listed contact is the Chemical Substances Examination Office, Chemical Substances Safety Division, Environmental Health Department, Minister's Secretariat, Ministry of the Environment.

Business Impact

Businesses handling the specified products containing PFOS or its salts and other covered substances may be affected by changes to the information displayed on containers, packaging or invoices. The supplied information does not identify the precise labeling changes or full product scope.
