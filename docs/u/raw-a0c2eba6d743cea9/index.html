Summary

This is a public comment (Japanese 'public comment' / opinion solicitation) on a draft amendment concerning labeling requirements for containers, packaging, or invoices of products that use PFOS or its salts, which are designated as a Class I Specified Chemical Substance under the Enforcement Order of the Act on the Regulation of Manufacture and Evaluation of Chemical Substances. According to the metadata, the draft was posted on 2026-09-01 with a comment deadline of 2026-10-01, and the contact is the Chemical Substances Safety Office of the Ministry of the Environment. It concerns measures to prevent environmental pollution caused by such substances and is at the proposal/consultation stage.

Business Impact

Businesses that manufacture, import, or handle products containing PFOS or its salts may be affected by potential changes to required labeling on containers, packaging, or invoices. The outcome could influence compliance and documentation practices, but details remain subject to the final text.
