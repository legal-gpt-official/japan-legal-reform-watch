Summary

Cabinet Order No. 308 of 2026 adjusts related Cabinet Orders in connection with partial implementation of an Act amending the Ministry of Defense Establishment Act and other laws. The listed amendment concerns the Enforcement Order of the National Public Officers Mutual Aid Association Act (Cabinet Order No. 207 of 1958); the metadata gives a promulgation date of 2026-09-30 and an effective date of 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

National public officers and their mutual aid associations may be affected because the listed amendment concerns their mutual aid enforcement order, rather than solely the organization of the Ministry of Defense. The metadata does not identify the provisions changed or their practical effects.
