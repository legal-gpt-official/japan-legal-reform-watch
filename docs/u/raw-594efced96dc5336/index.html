Summary

The Japan Exchange Group (JPX) has published a public comment item, attributed to the Osaka Exchange (OSE), regarding proposed expansion of daily price fluctuation limits for gold futures and related transactions. The consultation is out for public comment, with a stated submission period from September 24, 2026 to October 24, 2026. This is a proposal open for comment, not a finalized rule.

Business Impact

Firms trading gold futures or related products on the OSE may be affected by changes to daily price limit ranges, which could influence risk management and margin considerations.
