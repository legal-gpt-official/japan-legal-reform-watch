Summary

The Ministry of the Environment (Water and Atmospheric Environment Bureau) has opened a public comment period on a draft partial amendment to standards governing methods for modifying land where soil that does not meet soil elution standards is in contact with an aquifer within designated action-required areas. According to the metadata, the draft was announced on 2026/09/03 with a submission deadline of 2026/10/03, and it is categorized under environmental conservation. This is a draft out for public comment, not a finalized or enacted rule.

Business Impact

Parties involved in land use, remediation, or development on contaminated sites may be affected if the proposed standards for land modification methods are later adopted.
