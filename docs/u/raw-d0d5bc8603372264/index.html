Summary

Act No. 28 of 2019 amends the Payment Services Act and related laws to address diversification in financial transactions accompanying advances in information and communications technology. This listing concerns its amendment of the Long-Term Credit Bank Act (Act No. 187 of 1952); the stated promulgation date is 2019-06-07 and the stated effective date is 2020-05-01.

Business Impact

Long-term credit banks may be affected by the amendment to the Long-Term Credit Bank Act, rather than solely by the headline changes concerning payment services. The listing does not identify the amended provisions or their operational effects.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese source for the specific Long-Term Credit Bank Act provisions amended and any related transitional arrangements.
