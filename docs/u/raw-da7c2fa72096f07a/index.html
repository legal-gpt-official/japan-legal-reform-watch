摘要

2025年第341号政令规定，配合修订《出入国管理及难民认定法》和《外国人技能实习适当实施及技能实习生保护法》的法律施行，对相关政令进行调整并规定过渡措施。本条目涉及该政令对《统计法施行令》（2008年第334号政令）的修订，所列公布日期为2025-10-01，生效日期为2027-04-01。

Cabinet Order No. 341 of 2025 provides for adjustments to related Cabinet Orders and transitional measures accompanying implementation of an Act amending immigration and refugee recognition legislation and the law on proper technical intern training and protection of technical intern trainees. This listing concerns its amendment of the Order for Enforcement of the Statistics Act (Cabinet Order No. 334 of 2008), with a stated promulgation date of 2025-10-01 and effective date of 2027-04-01.
