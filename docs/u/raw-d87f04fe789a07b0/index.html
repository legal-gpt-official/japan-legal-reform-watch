Summary

Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry Ordinance No. 72 of 2026 amends the Rules on Examinations for High-Pressure Gas Production Safety Managers under the High Pressure Gas Safety Act (Ministry of International Trade and Industry Ordinance No. 54 of 1966) and the enforcement regulations under the Act on Securing Safety and Optimizing Transactions of Liquefied Petroleum Gas. The listed promulgation date is 2026-09-30, and the listed effective date is 2026-10-01.

Business Impact
