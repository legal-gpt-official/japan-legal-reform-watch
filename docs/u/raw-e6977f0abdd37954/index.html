Summary

Per a Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) press release dated 2026-07-27, the first joint meeting of the Energy and Economic Security Subcommittee will be held. The subcommittee sits under the Environment Committees of the Social Infrastructure Development Council and the Transport Policy Council's Transport System Subcommittee. The stated purpose is to discuss improving energy autonomy using mobility and infrastructure.
