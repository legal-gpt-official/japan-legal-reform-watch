Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 143 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with an effective date of 2027-03-01. It amends the rules governing treatment by insurance medical institutions and physicians, and by insurance pharmacies and pharmacists; the listed target is the Rules for Medical Care by Insurance Pharmacies and Insurance Pharmacists (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 16 of 1957).

Business Impact

Insurance medical institutions, insurance physicians, insurance pharmacies and insurance pharmacists may be affected by changes to their treatment rules. This listing specifically concerns the rules for insurance pharmacies and insurance pharmacists, but does not describe the substantive changes.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese ordinance could clarify the amended provisions, their scope and any transitional arrangements for the 2027-03-01 effective date.
