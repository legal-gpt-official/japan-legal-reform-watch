Summary

Japan's Forestry Agency (Rinya-cho) has opened a public comment period seeking opinions and information on a draft 'Comprehensive Supervisory Guidelines for Forestry Cooperatives, Production Forestry Cooperatives, and Forestry Cooperative Federations.' The notice was published on 2026-09-14, with a submission deadline of 2026-10-13 at 23:59. The contact listed is the Management Division, Forestry Administration Department of the Forestry Agency.

Business Impact

Organizations operating as or working with forestry cooperatives and their federations may find that the proposed supervisory guidelines could affect governance or oversight expectations if finalized.

Recommended Action

Consider reviewing the official e-Gov public comment notice and the underlying draft guidelines to confirm scope and submission details before the stated deadline.
