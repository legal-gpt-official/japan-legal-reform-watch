Cabinet Order No. 305 of 2026 amends related Cabinet Orders in connection with implementation of the Disaster Management Agency Establishment Act and its accompanying legislative adjustment Act. The listed amendment concerns the Order for Enforcement of the Act on Management of National Government Claims, Cabinet Order No. 337 of 1956; the stated promulgation date is 2026-09-30 and the effective date is 2026-11-02.
