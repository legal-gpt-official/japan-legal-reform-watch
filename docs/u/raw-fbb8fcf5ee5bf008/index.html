FSA announcement: first study group meeting on sound use of AI

摘要

金融厅于2026-11-25宣布公布促进人工智能等健全利用研究会首次会议的议程；该会议原定于2026-11-24举行。秘书处材料概述了金融机构的人工智能治理和风险管理问题，包括模型验证与监测、供应商选择、信息泄露、可解释性、偏见和幻觉。材料还指出了训练数据和提示词中涉及个人信息的问题，以及证券公司根据《金融商品交易法》使用生成式人工智能开展销售支持和投资劝诱的问题。参考材料概述了有关人工智能代理支付风险及资本市场人工智能监管的国际工作。这些是研究会讨论材料，并非关于新增约束性要求的公告。

对企业的影响

使用人工智能的金融机构，尤其是使用生成式人工智能开展销售支持或投资劝诱的证券公司，可能会发现相关讨论与其治理、客户信息处理和模型风险控制有关。支付服务提供商和支付系统运营商也可能会发现，参考材料与人工智能代理支付的授权、可审计性和责任有关。

建议采取的措施

可考虑开展审查，将人工智能模型清单、验证与监测、供应商数据使用控制和提示词限制，与秘书处材料中指出的问题进行比较。证券公司可另行研究有关非公开信息、法人相关信息管控，以及人工智能支持的活动是否构成劝诱的讨论。

Summary

The Financial Services Agency announced publication of the agenda for the first Study Group on Promoting the Sound Use of AI and Related Technologies on 2026-11-25; the meeting was scheduled for 2026-11-24. Secretariat materials summarize financial institutions' AI governance and risk-management issues, including model validation and monitoring, vendor selection, information leakage, explainability, bias and hallucinations. They also identify issues concerning personal information in training data and prompts, and securities firms' use of generative AI for sales support and investment solicitation under the Financial Instruments and Exchange Act. Reference materials summarize international work on AI-agent payment risks and AI supervision in capital markets. These are study-group discussion materials, not an announcement of new binding requirements.

Business Impact

Financial institutions using AI, particularly securities firms using generative AI for sales support or investment solicitation, may find the discussion relevant to their governance, customer-information handling and model-risk controls. Payment service providers and payment system operators may also find the reference material relevant to authorization, auditability and responsibility for AI-agent payments.

Recommended Action

A possible review could compare AI model inventories, validation and monitoring, vendor data-use controls and prompt restrictions with the issues identified in the secretariat materials. Securities firms could separately examine the discussion of non-public information, corporate-related information controls and whether AI-supported activities constitute solicitation.
