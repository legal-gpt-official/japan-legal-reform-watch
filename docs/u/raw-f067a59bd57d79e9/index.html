Summary

Cabinet Secretariat Ordinance No. 10 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-10-22, with an effective date of 2026-11-02. It amends the ordinance prescribing forms for recruiting and approving early-retirement applicants under the National Public Employees Retirement Allowance Act, and Cabinet Secretariat Ordinance No. 3 of 2022 concerning special rules for basic retirement allowance amounts under paragraphs 12, 14 and 16 of that Act's supplementary provisions.
