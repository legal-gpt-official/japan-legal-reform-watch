Summary

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism published a draft ministerial ordinance for public comment on 2026-08-14 to amend the Passenger Motor Transport Business Transportation Regulations and the ministry's implementing regulations on private businesses' use of information and communications technology for document retention and related activities. The stated comment deadline was 2026/09/13 at 09:30; the listed contact was the Passenger Transport Division, Logistics and Road Transport Bureau.

Business Impact

Passenger motor transport operators and private businesses covered by the ministry's electronic document-retention regulations may be affected by changes to transport and recordkeeping rules, but the specific proposed changes are not described in the available information.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese draft and consultation materials to check the amended provisions, their scope, and any proposed implementation arrangements.
