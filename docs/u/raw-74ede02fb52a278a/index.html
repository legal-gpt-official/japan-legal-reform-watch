Summary

The Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries published a draft amendment concerning florylpicoxamid to Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Notice No. 1650 of 2022 for public comment on 2026-10-26. The notice establishes ministerial criteria relating to Article 4, paragraph 1, item 5 of the Agricultural Chemicals Regulation Act; the stated comment deadline is 2026-11-24 23:59 JST. The listed contact is the Agricultural Produce Safety Management Division of the Food Safety and Consumer Affairs Bureau, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries.
