Joint Ministerial Ordinance amending public safety consultation rules for regional transport plans

要約

地域公共交通に関する各種実施計画等の認定に係る都道府県公安委員会の意見聴取を定める平成十九年内閣府・国土交通省令第二号を一部改正する命令（令和八年内閣府・国土交通省令第五号）が、2026年9月18日に公布され、施行日は2026年10月16日とされています。提供情報には、具体的な改正内容は記載されていません。

摘要

内阁府、国土交通省2026年第5号省令修订了两部门2007年第2号省令，该省令涉及依据《地区公共交通活性化及再生法》认定计划时征求都道府县公安委员会意见的程序。这些计划涵盖道路运输高度化实施、地区旅客运输服务持续实施、客货运输效率化实施、地区公共交通便利增进实施及新地区旅客运输业务。所载公布日期为2026-09-18，生效日期为2026-10-16。

Summary

Cabinet Office and Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism Ordinance No. 5 of 2026 amends their Ordinance No. 2 of 2007 on obtaining prefectural public safety commission opinions for certification of plans under the Act on Revitalization and Rehabilitation of Local Public Transportation. The plans cover advanced road transport implementation, continuation of regional passenger transport services, freight-passenger transport efficiency, regional public transport convenience improvements, and new regional passenger transport businesses. The stated promulgation date is 2026-09-18 and the effective date is 2026-10-16.

Business Impact

Operators involved in the five specified types of regional transport plans may be affected by changes to the public safety commission consultation procedures used in plan certification; the metadata does not describe the procedural changes.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese ordinance could clarify the amended consultation provisions and their application from 2026-10-16.
