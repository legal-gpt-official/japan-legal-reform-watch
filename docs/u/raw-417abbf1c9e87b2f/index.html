Summary

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) issued a press release announcing that a specialist subcommittee on the minimum wage for the bonito and tuna fishing industry will meet to deliberate on a revision. The subcommittee falls under the Seamen's Subcommittee of the Maritime Affairs Division of the Transport Policy Council. The item is a government announcement of a deliberation meeting; no revised wage figure or adoption is indicated in the provided metadata.
