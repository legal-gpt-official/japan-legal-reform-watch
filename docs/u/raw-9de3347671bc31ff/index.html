Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 110 of 2026 amends the Pharmacists Act Enforcement Regulations (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 5 of 1961). The ordinance adjusts ministry ordinances in connection with partial implementation of an Act amending the Act on Securing Quality, Efficacy and Safety of Products Including Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices and other laws. The metadata gives a promulgation date of 2026-06-30 and an effective date of 2027-05-20.
