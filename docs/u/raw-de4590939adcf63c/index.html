摘要

2026年第87号内阁府令配合《医疗法等部分修订法》的部分施行，对儿童家庭厅相关内阁府令进行整理。该府令修订了《关于医疗信息基础设施及医疗费用审查支付机构支援缴款业务财务及会计的内阁府令》（2024年第116号内阁府令）。所载公布日期为2026-09-30，生效日期为2026-10-01。

对企业的影响

医疗信息基础设施及医疗费用审查支付机构的支援缴款业务财务及会计可能受到影响；所述修订涉及这一较窄范围的事项，而非一般医疗服务提供者。

Summary

Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 87 of 2026 reorganizes Children and Families Agency-related Cabinet Office ordinances in connection with partial implementation of an Act amending the Medical Care Act and other laws. It amends the Cabinet Office Ordinance on Finance and Accounting for Support Contribution Operations of the Medical Information Infrastructure and Medical Fee Review and Payment Organization (Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 116 of 2024). The stated promulgation date is 2026-09-30 and the effective date is 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

The Medical Information Infrastructure and Medical Fee Review and Payment Organization may be affected in its finance and accounting for support contribution operations; the identified amendment concerns this narrower subject rather than healthcare providers generally.
