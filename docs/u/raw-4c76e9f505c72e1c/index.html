Summary

On 2026-11-19, the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare published an announcement that the Tokyo Labour Bureau had issued a worker-dispatch business improvement order against Medical World Co., Ltd. under Article 49(1) of the Worker Dispatching Act. The bureau found that, from at least 2024-10-01 through 2025-06-27, the company conducted dispatch operations totaling 362 person-days for duties covered by Article 2(1)(vii) of the Act's Enforcement Order without a statutory exception, violating Article 4(1) of the Act. The visible portion of the order requires a comprehensive review of all worker-dispatch, contracting and secondment operations for violations of the Worker Dispatching Act and the Employment Security Act. It also calls for prompt correction of identified violations, with measures to stabilize workers' employment as a prerequisite.
