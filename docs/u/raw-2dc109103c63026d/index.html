摘要

环境省于2026-11-12宣布就濒危野生动植物保护答申草案公开征求意见，所列意见征集期为2026-11-12至2026-12-11。草案涉及栖息地保护、避免和减少开发影响、与私营主体建立保护伙伴关系、加强中央与地方政府协调，以及调整转让相关监管。该草案是在对2018年6月生效的一部修正法进行法定审查，并经中央环境审议会野生生物小委员会讨论后提出的。公告还将此次审查与2025年12月的大规模太阳能政策组合相联系，包括推动设立栖息地保护区，以及考虑针对影响稀有物种的开发活动采取有效应对措施；这些内容属于答申草案，并非已制定生效的变更。

Summary

The Ministry of the Environment announced a public consultation on draft recommendations for endangered wild fauna and flora conservation on 2026-11-12, with a stated comment period of November 12 to December 11, 2026. The draft addresses habitat conservation, avoidance and reduction of development impacts, conservation partnerships with private actors, stronger national-local government coordination, and adjustments to transfer-related regulation. It follows a statutory review of an amending Act that took effect in June 2018 and discussions by the Central Environment Council's Wildlife Subcommittee. The announcement also links the review to the December 2025 mega-solar policy package, including promotion of habitat protection areas and consideration of effective responses to development affecting rare species; these are draft recommendations, not enacted changes.

Developers, including large-scale solar power developers, and parties involved in transfers of endangered wild fauna and flora may be affected by future measures on development impacts and transfer-related regulation. Private actors participating in conservation activities could also be concerned by the proposed partnership measures, but the excerpt does not establish specific new obligations.
