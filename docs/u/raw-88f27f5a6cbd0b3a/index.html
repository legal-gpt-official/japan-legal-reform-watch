Summary

This item, listed in the e-Gov Law Search update list, is an amending ordinance (MLIT Ordinance No. 73 of Reiwa 8) that partially revises the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism ordinance on certification of supply assurance plans under the economic security promotion act (MLIT Ordinance No. 93 of Reiwa 4).
