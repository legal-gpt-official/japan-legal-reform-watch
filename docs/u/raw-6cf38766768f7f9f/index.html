Ministry of Defense Ordinance No. 24 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with an effective date of 2026-10-01, to adjust related ministerial ordinances following partial implementation of the Act Partially Amending the Ministry of Defense Establishment Act and Other Acts.
