Summary

A public consultation concerns draft designations by the ministers responsible for special social infrastructure businesses and the Prime Minister under the proviso to Article 2(1) of the Order on Reporting Specified Intrusion Incidents by Special Social Infrastructure Businesses under the Act on Preventing Damage from Unauthorized Acts against Important Computers. The draft was announced on 2026/07/21, with a comment deadline of 2026/08/25 00:00; the listed contact is the Cabinet Office office supporting the Director-General for Cybersecurity.
