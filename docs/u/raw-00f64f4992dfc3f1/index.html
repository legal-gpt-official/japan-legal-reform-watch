Summary

The National Tax Agency announced on 2026-03-31 Notice No. 8 amending National Tax Agency Notice No. 18 of 2021. The amended notice designates applications and related submissions specified by the NTA Commissioner under Article 5-2, paragraph 1 of the ministerial ordinance on promoting the use of information and communications technology in national-tax administration.
