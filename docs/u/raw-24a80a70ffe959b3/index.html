Summary

This is a Cabinet Order (Reiwa 7 Cabinet Order No. 341) that arranges related cabinet orders and sets transitional measures in connection with the enforcement of a law partially amending the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the law on proper implementation of technical intern training and protection of technical intern trainees. The metadata indicates a promulgation date of 2025-10-01 and a stated enforcement date of 2027-04-01. One affected order noted is the Enforcement Order of the National Public Officers Mutual Aid Association Act (Showa 33 Cabinet Order No. 207).

Business Impact

Organizations involved in foreign worker programs, technical intern training, or immigration processes could be affected by consequential adjustments to related regulations once the changes take effect.
