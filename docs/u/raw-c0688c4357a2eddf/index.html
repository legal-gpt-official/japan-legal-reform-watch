摘要

2026-07-13公布的公开征求意见涉及特别社会基础设施运营者依据《防止针对重要电子计算机的不正当行为造成损害法》报告特定侵害事件所用的格式草案及相关材料。这些格式拟由特别社会基础设施业务主管大臣及内阁总理大臣依据《关于特别社会基础设施运营者报告特定侵害事件等事项的命令》第四条第二款规定；联系部门为内阁府负责网络安全的政策协调办公室。意见提交截止时间为2026-08-17 00:00。

Summary

A public consultation announced on 2026/07/13 concerns draft forms and related materials for designated critical infrastructure operators reporting specified infringement incidents under the Act on Prevention of Damage Caused by Unauthorized Acts against Important Computers. The forms would be prescribed by the ministers responsible for designated critical infrastructure businesses and the Prime Minister under Article 4(2) of the reporting order; the contact office is the Cabinet Office's policy coordination office responsible for cyber security. The comment deadline is 2026/08/17 00:00.

Business Impact

Designated critical infrastructure operators may be affected because the proposed forms concern their reporting of specified infringement incidents and related matters.

Recommended Action

Review of the official consultation materials could clarify the proposed forms, the reporting information requested, and the comment submission requirements before the stated deadline.
