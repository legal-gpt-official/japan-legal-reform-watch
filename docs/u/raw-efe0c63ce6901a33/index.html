Summary

Cabinet Order No. 340 of 2026 amends the Cabinet Order on provisional anti-dumping duties on nickel-based stainless steel cold-rolled strip and sheet. The listing records promulgation on 2026-10-30 and an effective date of 2026-10-31. The updated instrument identified in the listing is the Enforcement Order of the Act on Processing of Import-Export Related Business through an Electronic Data Processing System, Cabinet Order No. 220 of 1977; the specific amendment to that order is not provided.

Business Impact

Importers of the named steel products may be affected by the anti-dumping duty amendment. However, this listing concerns an update to the order governing electronic processing of import-export procedures, and does not establish any change to duty rates, product coverage or filing requirements.
