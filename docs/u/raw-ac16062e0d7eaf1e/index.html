摘要

2022年第299号政令配合《促进定居等及地区间交流以振兴农山渔村法》的修订法实施，对相关政令作出调整。所列修订涉及《补助金等相关预算适当执行确保法施行令》（1955年第255号政令）；载明的公布日期为2022-09-07，生效日期为2022-10-01。

对企业的影响

受补助金预算执行规则涵盖的补助金领取者可能受到此次配套修订的影响，而不只是参与农山渔村振兴的主体；具体变更及受影响的补助金类别未作说明。

Summary

Cabinet Order No. 299 of 2022 adjusts related Cabinet Orders in connection with implementation of an Act amending the law on promoting settlement and interregional exchange for revitalizing agricultural, mountain and fishing villages. The listed amendment concerns the Enforcement Order of the Act on Ensuring Proper Execution of Budgets Relating to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955); the stated promulgation date is 2022-09-07 and the effective date is 2022-10-01.

Business Impact

Recipients of subsidies covered by the subsidy budget execution rules may be affected by this consequential amendment, rather than solely those involved in rural revitalization; the specific changes and affected subsidy categories are not described.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese text could clarify which subsidy budget execution provisions were amended and how they apply from 2022-10-01.
