Summary

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) issued a press release stating that deliberation will be held on revising the minimum wage for the nationwide coastal steel vessel operation industry. According to the title, a specialist committee on the minimum wage for this industry, under the Seamen's Subcommittee of the Maritime Subcommittee of the Transport Policy Council, will meet. This is a government announcement of a meeting; no finalized revision is indicated in the provided metadata.

If a revision to the minimum wage for this sector is later proposed or adopted, it could affect wage levels and labor costs for operators and seafarers in coastal steel vessel operations. The provided information does not indicate what changes, if any, are under consideration.
