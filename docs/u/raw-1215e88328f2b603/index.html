Summary

This item is an amending act (Reiwa 1, Act No. 21) that partially revises the Act on Strengthening the Management of Small and Medium-sized Enterprises (Heisei 11, Act No. 18) and related laws, aimed at supporting the continuity of business activities of small and medium-sized enterprises. According to the provided metadata, the promulgation date is listed as 2019-06-05 and the enforcement date as 2019-07-16.
