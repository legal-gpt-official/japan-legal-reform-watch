Summary

The Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry published a draft ministerial ordinance for public comment on 2026-11-20. It would amend the ordinance setting the amounts required per unit quantity for Type 1 and Type 2 final disposal operations under Article 11(3) and Article 11-2(3) of the Act on Final Disposal of Specified Radioactive Waste. The stated comment deadline is 2026-12-21 00:00 JST; the supplied information does not specify the proposed amounts or an effective date.

Business Impact

Entities funding Type 1 or Type 2 final disposal of specified radioactive waste may face changes in the per-unit amounts used to determine disposal funding. The supplied listing does not establish the direction or size of any change.

Recommended Action

A review could compare the draft's Type 1 and Type 2 per-unit amounts with the existing ordinance and check the proposed application date before assessing any funding implications.
