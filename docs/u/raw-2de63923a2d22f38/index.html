Summary

Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) has opened a call for public comments on a draft evaluation of the degree of efficient radio spectrum use, based on the FY2025 (Reiwa 7) radio usage survey. The draft covers various wireless systems and public-service (public-business) radio stations. This is a draft evaluation result open for public comment, not a finalized or binding measure.
