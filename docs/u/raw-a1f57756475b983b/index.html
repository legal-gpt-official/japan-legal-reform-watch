摘要

Summary

This MHLW ministerial ordinance (Reiwa 8 MHLW Ordinance No. 18, promulgated 2026-02-26) makes conforming changes to ministerial ordinances in connection with the enforcement of an amending act to the Labor Measures Comprehensive Promotion Act and related laws. The e-Gov update list shows it amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on Securing, Etc. of Equal Opportunity and Treatment between Men and Women in Employment (Ministry of Labour Ordinance No. 2 of 1986). The listed enforcement date is 2026-10-01.
