Summary

This is a National Tax Agency (NTA) notice that partially amends an earlier NTA notification (Reiwa 8 NTA Notice No. 4) which specifies file formats designated by the Commissioner of the NTA under various tax-related ministerial ordinances, including provisions of the ordinance promoting the use of information and communications technology for national-tax administration, and rules under the Corporation Tax Act, Local Corporation Tax Act, Consumption Tax Act, and the ordinance on special corporate defense tax.
