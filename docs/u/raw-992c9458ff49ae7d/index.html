Summary

This item is a Cabinet Order (Reiwa 7 Cabinet Order No. 341) that adjusts related Cabinet Orders and sets transitional measures in connection with the enforcement of the act amending the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Technical Intern Training Act. According to the metadata, it was promulgated on 2025-10-01 with an enforcement date of 2027-04-01, and it appears in the e-Gov update list as an amendment to the Order for Enforcement of the National Strategic Special Zones Act (Heisei 26 Cabinet Order No. 99).
