摘要

日本厚生劳动省就一项告示草案公开征求意见，该草案拟部分修订有关指定认定机构认定的专科医师资格的规定。相关规定涉及《疑难病患者医疗等相关法施行规则》和《儿童福利法施行规则》。根据元数据，草案于2026-09-19公布，意见提交截止日期为2026-10-19。

对企业的影响

如果修订改变获认可的认定机构或资格要求，参与疑难病或儿童福利医疗专科医师资格认定的组织或专业人员可能会受到影响。

Summary

This is a public comment invitation from Japan's Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare regarding a draft notice that would partly amend provisions on specialist qualifications certified by designated certification bodies. The provisions relate to the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on Medical Care for Patients with Intractable Diseases and the Enforcement Regulations of the Child Welfare Act. According to the metadata, the draft was announced on 2026-09-19 with a comment deadline of 2026-10-19.

Business Impact

Organizations or professionals involved in certifying medical specialists for intractable disease or child welfare medical care may be affected if the amendment changes recognized certification bodies or qualification requirements.
