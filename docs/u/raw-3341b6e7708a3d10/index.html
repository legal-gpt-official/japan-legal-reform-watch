Summary

Cabinet Order No. 4 of 2019 adjusts related Cabinet Orders following the implementation of an Act amending legislation on strengthening research and development capacity and promoting efficient research and development. The listed amended instrument is the Enforcement Order of the Act on Regulation of Execution of Budget Pertaining to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955); the stated promulgation and effective dates are both 2019-01-17.
