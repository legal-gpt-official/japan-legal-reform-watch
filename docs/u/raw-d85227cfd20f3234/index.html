要約

地方公共団体の標準化対象事務のうち、所定の事務処理に係るシステムに必要な機能等の標準化基準を定める内閣府令の一部改正案が、2026年7月24日に公示されました。意見募集の受付締切は2026年8月6日23:59 JSTで、問合せ先はこども家庭庁成育局保育政策課企画法令第一係です。

Summary

This is a public comment invitation on a draft Cabinet Office Ordinance that would partially amend rules setting standardization criteria for functions required by systems handling certain tasks under the Act on Standardization of Local Government Information Systems. The draft was published on 2026-07-24, with comments accepted until 2026-08-06 at 23:59. The inquiry contact is listed as the Children and Families Agency, Growth and Development Bureau, Childcare Policy Division.

Business Impact

Organizations involved with local government information systems or related childcare/social welfare administration may be affected if the draft standardization criteria are adopted.
