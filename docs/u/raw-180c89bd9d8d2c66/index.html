At a glance

What Changed?

Cabinet Order No. 329 of 2026 took effect on 1 November. It delegates part of the minister's authority over approval of joint truck relay-transport implementation plans to regional transport bureau directors and makes related technical cross-reference amendments. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Who Is Affected?

Truck operators planning multi-driver relay routes and facilities may use the certification regime. Regional transport bureaus administer delegated approvals; the decree does not impose a new blanket obligation on all shippers. [1] [2] [3] [4]

What Should Businesses Do Now?

For a proposed relay-transport plan, establish the operating area and which transport bureau has delegated authority. Check the statutory certification criteria and application guidance separately from this delegation decree. [1] [2] [3] [4]

Background & Purpose

Japan's logistics network depends on road freight, but the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism identifies the ageing of truck drivers, labour shortages and working-time constraints introduced in April 2024 as pressures on long-distance transport capacity. Traditionally, one driver could undertake an entire lengthy journey, making compliance with working-time limits and a sustainable workforce harder to reconcile.

The 2026 statutory response was to promote relay transport: multiple drivers share a long route, potentially through handover locations, rather than one person completing the entire trip. The statutory reform created a framework for joint relay-transport implementation plans and government certification, reflecting the need for cooperation among carriers and other participants and suitable shared facilities. The statute's cooperation provisions should not be confused with an unconditional duty to obtain certification.

Cabinet Order No. 329 operationalises the statute by delegating part of the transport minister's certification and related powers to the head of the regional transport bureau covering the area in which the relay transport operates. It also adjusts article cross-references in a separate SME Support Agency enforcement order. Those technical cross-reference amendments do not establish a second, general relay-transport licensing mandate.

Legislative & Regulatory History

On 6 March 2026 the Cabinet approved the relay-transport amendment bill. MLIT described the pressures created by driver demographics, insufficient staffing and the April 2024 working-time reforms, and proposed a basic policy, cooperation and a certification route to support collective transport plans and access to relay facilities.

The Logistics Efficiency Act amendment, Act No. 21 of 2026, was promulgated on 20 May. The Cabinet then approved the implementing delegation order and a separate commencement order on 16 October. The decree was promulgated on 21 October and took effect on 1 November 2026, the same date fixed for commencement of the amendment statute.

The draft order underwent consultation from 26 August through the beginning of 25 September; e-Gov recorded zero submitted comments and no change attributable to comments. The publication record supports the decree's adoption and dates, not a claim that there was consensus among all affected businesses.

Business Impact & Practical Considerations

Carriers contemplating a shared long-distance operation can assess whether a joint relay plan is appropriate and which regional transport bureau covers its operating area. For activities falling within delegated authority, the decision-maker may be a regional bureau director rather than the minister directly. Certification and access to any assistance are governed by the statute and relevant rules, not by the delegation provision alone.

Shippers, logistics-facility operators and software or dispatch providers may be counterparties to planned relay arrangements, but this particular decree is about administrative delegation and formal cross-reference maintenance. Its entry into force should not be portrayed as a new universal reporting, certification or payment obligation for every business in the supply chain.

Points to check

  • Map each contemplated relay-transport project's operating region and identify the regional transport bureau competent for delegated plan certification.
  • Review joint-plan participants, proposed handover locations and potential statutory assistance separately under the amendment statute and application guidelines.
  • Do not treat technical amendments to the SME agency order or the delegation decree as a blanket licensing or reporting duty for shippers.
