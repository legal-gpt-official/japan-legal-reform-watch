Summary

This item is an amending order that partially revises the existing order governing the business operations, finance and accounting, and personnel management of Japan's Information-technology Promotion Agency (IPA), an independent administrative agency. According to the provided metadata, the base order is Ordinance No. 78 of the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (2020), and the amending order is Ordinance No. 1 of 2026 issued jointly by the Digital Agency and the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry.
