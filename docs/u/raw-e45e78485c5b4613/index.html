Background & Purpose

Japan's Foreign Exchange and Foreign Trade Act provides the legal basis for controls on exports of specified goods and certain cross-border technology transactions. METI's October 2026 consultation concerns proposed changes to the implementing Cabinet Orders, a ministerial ordinance, a notice and related administrative circulars.

The draft Export Trade Control Order revises the treatment of nucleic-acid synthesis and joining equipment, adds certain additive-manufacturing equipment used for explosive-related products, replaces a sampling-oscilloscope category with a real-time-oscilloscope category, and removes an underwater-lighting category. Detailed technical coverage depends on the companion ministerial ordinance.

The draft Foreign Exchange Order also proposes changes to controlled technology categories, an additional exception concerning payments before required approvals for specified exports, and a revised definition of foreign-bound temporarily landed cargo. These are proposed changes, not current compliance exemptions or obligations.
