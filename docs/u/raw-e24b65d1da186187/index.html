Summary

The Ministry of Defense announced a public consultation on 2026/08/07 on draft rules concerning concurrent employment of Self-Defense Forces reservists and related personnel under Article 3(1) of the Act on Special Exceptions to Concurrent Employment for National and Local Public Employees to Facilitate Reserve Service. The comment deadline is 2026/09/05 23:59; the responsible office is the Human Resources Development Division, Personnel and Education Bureau.

Business Impact

The draft may concern national and local public employees serving as Self-Defense Forces reservists or related personnel because it addresses concurrent employment in connection with reserve duties. The available information does not establish the proposed requirements.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese consultation materials would help clarify the covered personnel, proposed concurrent-employment rules, and comment submission arrangements.
