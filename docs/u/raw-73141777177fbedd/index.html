Summary

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 71 of 2026, amending the enforcement regulations on stabilizing staple food supply, demand and prices and other regulations, was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with an effective date of 2027-04-01. The listed target is the Enforcement Regulations of the Act on Granting Payments for Management Stability to Agricultural Producers, Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 59 of 2006.
