Summary

Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications Ordinance No. 108 of 2026 amends Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications Ordinance No. 51 of 2026, which sets standardization criteria for functions and other requirements of local government information systems handling specified administrative tasks. The scope covers tasks listed in Article 6 of the order specifying administrative tasks under the local government information system standardization framework. The amending ordinance was promulgated on 2026-08-31 and took effect on 2026-08-31.

Business Impact

Local governments handling the specified administrative tasks may be affected by changes to the standardization criteria for their information systems; the supplied information does not identify the detailed changes.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese text could clarify which system requirements and Article 6 tasks are affected and whether transitional provisions apply.
