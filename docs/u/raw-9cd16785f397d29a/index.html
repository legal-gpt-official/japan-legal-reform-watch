摘要

2022年第348号政令于2022-11-11公布，规定了为确立稳定的能源供需结构而修订能源相关法律的部分规定施行时，对相关政令进行调整及采取过渡措施。所列修订涉及《关于补助金等预算执行适正化的法律施行令》（1955年第255号政令），注明的生效日期为2022-11-14。

Summary

Cabinet Order No. 348 of 2022, promulgated on 2022-11-11, provides for adjustments to related Cabinet Orders and transitional measures accompanying partial implementation of energy-law amendments aimed at establishing a stable energy supply-demand structure. The listed amendment concerns the Order for Enforcement of the Act on Ensuring Proper Execution of Budgets Relating to Subsidies, etc. (Cabinet Order No. 255 of 1955), with a stated effective date of 2022-11-14.

Business Impact

Recipients of subsidies covered by the subsidy budget execution framework may be affected by this consequential amendment, rather than energy-sector businesses generally. The metadata does not identify the amended provisions or their practical effects.
