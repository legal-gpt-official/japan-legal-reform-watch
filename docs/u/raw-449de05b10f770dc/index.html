Summary

On 2026-06-05, the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) announced its decision to lift the designations of former supply areas and related designations for former general gas utilities deemed to be gas retailers, effective October 1, 2026. The decision is based on Article 22(2) of the Supplementary Provisions of the Act Partially Amending the Electricity Business Act and Other Acts (Act No. 47 of 2015).

Business Impact

Former general gas utilities deemed to be gas retailers may be affected by the lifting of their former supply-area designations; the supplied text does not identify the specific retailers or areas covered.
