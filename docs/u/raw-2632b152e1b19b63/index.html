Summary

The Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare has opened a public comment period on a draft ministerial ordinance that would partially amend the enforcement regulations of the Act on Securing a Sanitary Environment in Buildings. According to the metadata, the draft was announced on 2026/09/16 and the comment period closes on 2026/10/16. This is a proposal out for public comment and has not been finalized or brought into force.
