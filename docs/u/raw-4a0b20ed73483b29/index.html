This item is a Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry (METI) ministerial order that amends the enforcement regulations under the Industrial Competitiveness Enhancement Act and the ministerial order defining requirements under Article 59(1) of the Act on the Corporation for Revitalizing Earthquake-Affected Businesses.
