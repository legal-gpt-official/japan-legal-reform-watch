摘要

2024年第119号厚生劳动省令修订了《社会保险劳务士法施行规则》（1968年第1号厚生省、劳动省令）。该省令配合修订《关于在行政程序中使用识别特定个人的号码等的法律》等法律的法律的部分施行，对厚生劳动省相关省令作出调整。所载公布日期为2024-08-30，所载生效日期为2024-12-02。

Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 119 of 2024 amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Labor and Social Security Attorney Act (Ministry of Health and Welfare and Ministry of Labour Ordinance No. 1 of 1968). It adjusts ministry-related ordinances in connection with partial implementation of legislation amending the law on using numbers to identify specific individuals in administrative procedures and other laws.
