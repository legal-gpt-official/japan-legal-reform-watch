Summary

Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 75 of 2026 amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Child Welfare Act (Ministry of Health and Welfare Ordinance No. 11 of 1948) and the Cabinet Office Ordinance on special formats for staff identification carried during on-site inspections and similar activities under laws administered by the Children and Families Agency. The listed promulgation date is 2026-09-07 and the effective date is 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

Child welfare service providers and entities subject to inspections under laws administered by the Children and Families Agency may be affected by changes to the enforcement regulations or inspection staff identification formats; the specific changes are not provided.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese ordinance to check the amended child welfare provisions, changes to inspection staff identification formats, and any transitional arrangements for 2026-10-01.
