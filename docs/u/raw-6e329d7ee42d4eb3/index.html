Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 29 of 2026 amends the Ordinance on Prevention of Hazards Due to Specified Chemical Substances (Ministry of Labour Ordinance No. 39 of 1972). It adjusts related ministerial ordinances in connection with partial implementation of the Act amending the Industrial Safety and Health Act and the Working Environment Measurement Act. The listing states a promulgation date of 2026-03-23 and an effective date of 2027-04-01.

Business Impact
