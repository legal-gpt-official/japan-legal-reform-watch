Summary

Act No. 74 of 2025, an Act to amend the National Pension Act and other laws to strengthen pension system functions in response to socioeconomic changes, amends the Private School Personnel Mutual Aid Act (Act No. 245 of 1953). The listing records promulgation on 2025-06-20 and an effective date of 2028-04-01 for this amendment.

Business Impact

Private school personnel covered by the mutual aid system may be affected by this amendment. Although the reform Act has a broader pension-related title, this listing specifically concerns the Private School Personnel Mutual Aid Act and does not describe the substantive changes.
