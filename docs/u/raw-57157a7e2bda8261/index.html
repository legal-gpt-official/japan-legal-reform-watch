Summary

Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) has opened a public comment period on a draft Cabinet Order. The draft would partially amend the (pre-repeal) Enforcement Order of the Diet Members' Mutual Aid Pension Act, which remains effective under a supplementary provision of the Cabinet Order that repealed the pension regulations and enforcement order. This is a draft out for public comment, not an enacted measure.

Business Impact

The proposal appears narrowly focused on pension-related enforcement provisions for Diet members and may have limited relevance to most private organizations.
