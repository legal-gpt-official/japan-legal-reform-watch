Summary

Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 67 of 2026 amends the ministry's Ordinance on Certification of Supply Security Plans under the Act on the Promotion of Ensuring National Security through Integrated Implementation of Economic Measures (Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries Ordinance No. 75 of 2022). The listing states that the amending ordinance was promulgated and took effect on 2026-09-18.

Business Impact

Applicants for certification of supply security plans within the Ministry of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries' remit may be affected by changes to the certification rules; the listing does not identify the amended requirements or covered supplies.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese ordinance could clarify the amended certification provisions and any transitional arrangements.
