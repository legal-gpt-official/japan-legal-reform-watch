Summary

This item concerns a MEXT (Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science and Technology) ministerial ordinance that reorganizes related ministerial rules in connection with the enforcement of a Cabinet Order partially amending the Financial Services Agency Organization Order. The affected regulation is identified as the ministerial ordinance on the Japan Student Services Organization (Heisei 16 MEXT Ordinance No. 23), amended by Reiwa 8 MEXT Ordinance No. 28. The metadata indicates a promulgation date of 2026-08-05 and an enforcement date of 2026-08-07.
