Summary

This item refers to a ministerial ordinance (Ordinance of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare No. 124 of 2026) setting standardization criteria for the functions required of systems handling affairs specified under Article 14 of the relevant order, under the Act on Standardization of Local Government Information Systems. According to the metadata, the promulgation date and enforcement date are both listed as 2026-08-07. The information comes from an e-Gov Law Search update listing.

Business Impact

Local governments and vendors that build or operate covered information systems may need to review how their systems align with the referenced standardization criteria.
