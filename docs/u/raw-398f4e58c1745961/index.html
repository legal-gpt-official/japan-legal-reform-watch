Summary

This is a Japanese Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism (MLIT) ordinance to adjust MLIT-related ministerial ordinances following amendments to laws on comprehensive labor policy promotion and worker employment stability. According to the metadata, it affects the Enforcement Regulations for the Act on Promotion of Employment of Persons with Disabilities Concerning Seafarers (Heisei 28 MLIT Ordinance No. 22). The ordinance carries the number Reiwa 8 MLIT Ordinance No. 72, with a promulgation date of 2026-08-25 and a stated enforcement date of 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

Organizations involved in maritime or transport-sector employment of seafarers, including matters relating to employment of persons with disabilities, may be affected by these updated regulatory provisions.
