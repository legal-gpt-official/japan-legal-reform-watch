Summary

Ministry of Justice Ordinance No. 45 of 2025, promulgated on 2025-09-30, provides for adjustments to ministry ordinances and transitional measures accompanying legislation amending immigration control and technical intern training laws. The listed amendment concerns the Ordinance Establishing Standards for Specified Skilled Worker Employment Contracts and Support Plans for Specified Skilled Worker (i) Foreign Nationals, Ministry of Justice Ordinance No. 5 of 2019; the stated effective date is 2027-04-01.

Business Impact

Employers entering into specified skilled worker employment contracts and those responsible for support plans for Specified Skilled Worker (i) foreign nationals may be affected by changes to the applicable standards or transitional arrangements. The listed target ordinance concerns these contracts and support plans, rather than the full scope of the immigration and technical intern training legislation named in the amending ordinance.

Recommended Action

Review of the official Japanese source could clarify the amended contract and support-plan standards, applicable transitional provisions, and their application from 2027-04-01.
