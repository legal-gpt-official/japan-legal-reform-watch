摘要

2025年法务省、厚生劳动省令第4号涉及《外国人育成就业适当实施及育成就业外国人保护法》的施行规则。该列表将其列为对2016年法务省、厚生劳动省令第3号《外国人技能实习适当实施及技能实习生保护法施行规则》的修订，公布日期为2025-09-30，生效日期为2027-04-01。

对企业的影响

从事育成就业的外国人、技能实习生以及在这些制度下开展业务的雇主，可能受到施行规则变更的影响。该列表未说明实质性要求。

建议采取的措施

可考虑在2027-04-01之前查阅日文官方省令，以明确修订条款、两项制度之间的关系以及任何过渡安排。

Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 4 of 2025 concerns rules implementing the Act on Proper Implementation of Employment for Skill Development of Foreign Nationals and Protection of Foreign Nationals in Employment for Skill Development. The listing identifies it as amending the rules implementing the Act on Proper Implementation of Technical Intern Training for Foreign Nationals and Protection of Technical Intern Trainees, Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 3 of 2016, with promulgation on 2025-09-30 and an effective date of 2027-04-01.
