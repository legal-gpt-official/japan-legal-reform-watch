Summary

Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 87 of 2026 was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with an effective date of 2026-10-01, to adjust Children and Families Agency-related Cabinet Office ordinances following partial implementation of the Act Partially Amending the Medical Care Act and Other Acts. The listed amendment concerns Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 115 of 2024, which specifies matters to include in the operating procedures document for support-contribution operations of the Medical Information Infrastructure and Medical Fee Review and Payment Organization.

Business Impact

The Medical Information Infrastructure and Medical Fee Review and Payment Organization may be affected through changes to requirements for its support-contribution operating procedures document; the listing does not describe the substantive changes.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese text for the amended operating-procedure requirements and any transitional provisions associated with the 2026-10-01 effective date.
