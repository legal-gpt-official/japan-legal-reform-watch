Summary

This is a public comment solicitation on a draft (proposed) notice that would partly amend the list of 'drugs requiring guidance' (yo-shido iyakuhin) designated by the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare under Article 4, paragraph 5, item 3 of the Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Act. The draft was announced on 2026/10/01 and comments are accepted until 2026/10/31 00:00. The inquiry contact is the Pharmaceutical Evaluation Division of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW).

Business Impact

If adopted, the amendment could change which drugs are designated as requiring guidance, which may affect how affected products are classified, sold, or labeled. The specific products and practical effects are not stated in the provided metadata.
