Summary

Act No. 32 of 2026 amends the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act (Cabinet Order No. 319 of 1951) and the Act on Special Provisions for Landing Applications by Foreign Nationals Holding Passports Specified in Article 2, Item 5(b) of that Act. The listed promulgation date is 2026-06-05, and the listed effective date is 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

The amendments may affect foreign nationals governed by immigration control rules, including those holding the specified passports who apply for landing under the special provisions. The metadata does not describe the substantive changes to those rules.
