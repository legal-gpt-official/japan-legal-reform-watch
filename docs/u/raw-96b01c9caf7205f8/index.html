Summary

Japan's Ministry of the Environment (Natural Environment Bureau, National Park Division) has opened a public comment period on a draft designating alpine and similar plants that may not be collected or damaged without permission within the special zones of Nikko National Park. According to the metadata, the draft was announced on 2026/07/21 with a comment submission deadline of 2026/08/21. This is a draft out for public comment and not a finalized or enforced measure.

Business Impact

If adopted, the designation could affect activities involving the collection or handling of specified plants within the special zones of Nikko National Park, and may create permit-related considerations for those operating there.
