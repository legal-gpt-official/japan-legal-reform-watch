Cabinet Order No. 268 of 2026 amends the Enforcement Order of the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Enforcement Order of the Special Act on the Immigration Control of Those Who Have Lost Japanese Nationality pursuant to the Treaty of Peace with Japan and Others.
