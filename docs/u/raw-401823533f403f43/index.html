Background & Purpose

Japan's rural renewable-energy scheme was designed to make renewable electricity compatible with the healthy development of agriculture, forestry and fisheries. In agrivoltaics, the key challenge is verifying that crop production continues beneath solar equipment and that the installation does not leave farmland with an unresolved removal burden.

The 2026 joint ordinance addresses this implementation problem through the information and undertakings accompanying a municipal project-recognition application. Its revised framework requires attention to the farming plan below the panels, removal and site restoration, and reporting of implementation. It complements, but is legally distinct from, the separate 2026 MAFF ordinance that changes the farmland permission rules.

Legislative & Regulatory History

A draft of the joint ordinance was published for consultation in July 2026. Joint Ordinance No. 8 was promulgated on 9 October 2026, and the e-Gov record shows commencement on that date. The Ministry has published revised application forms and its October operational guidance for municipalities and project sponsors.

The administrative focus has moved from merely identifying the generating equipment to demonstrating how the project fits the municipal plan, keeps cultivation viable and addresses its eventual removal. The updated form set includes farming-plan and decommissioning-related materials.

Business Impact & Practical Considerations

Developers seeking municipal recognition should budget for agronomic documentation, agreements on equipment removal and continuing reporting. Lenders and offtakers can use the recognized plan and undertakings as due-diligence evidence, but should not treat them as proof that every other permit has been obtained.

Municipalities gain more structured information about the cultivation and removal dimensions of a project. The mechanism seeks to reduce the risk that solar installations remain in place while the underlying farming obligation becomes ineffective.

Points to check

  • Use the current municipal project-recognition application and agrivoltaic farming-plan attachments.
  • Identify the party responsible for decommissioning and demonstrate how removal costs will be secured.
  • Check reporting commitments and separately verify any farmland conversion or other permits.
