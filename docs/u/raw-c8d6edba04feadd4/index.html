Summary

Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism Ordinance No. 83 of 2026 amends the Passenger Motor Transport Business Transportation Regulations and the ministry's rules implementing the Act on the Use of Information and Communications Technology in the Preservation of Documents by Private Businesses and Others. The latter rules are Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism Ordinance No. 26 of 2005; the stated promulgation and effective dates of the amendment are both 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

Passenger motor transport operators and private businesses and others preserving documents under laws administered by the ministry may be affected. This listing specifically concerns the electronic document-preservation rules, but does not identify the provisions changed or their operational effects.

Recommended Action

Review the official Japanese amendment for the changes to passenger transport and electronic document-preservation requirements, including any transitional provisions.
