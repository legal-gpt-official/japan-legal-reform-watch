Summary

The Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) has opened a public comment on a draft ordinance that would partially amend the ministerial ordinance setting standardization criteria for functions required of systems handling certain administrative tasks. These criteria relate to standardization of local government information systems under the relevant law and associated orders. This is a draft out for public comment, not an enacted rule.

Business Impact

Organizations involved in local government information systems may be affected by changes to required system functions and standardization criteria if the draft is adopted.
