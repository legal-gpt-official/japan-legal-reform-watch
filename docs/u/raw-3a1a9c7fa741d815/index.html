Cabinet Office Ordinance No. 90 of 2026 amends standards for the facilities and operation of child welfare facilities and after-school child sound development services. Operators of child welfare facilities and after-school child sound development services may be affected by the amendments named in the title. This particular update concerns standards for temporary protection facilities, whose operators may need to assess the changes to facility and operational requirements.
