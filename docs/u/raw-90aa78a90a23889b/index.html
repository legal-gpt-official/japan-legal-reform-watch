Summary

This is a public comment consultation on two draft amendments concerning the designation of certain non-prescription (OTC) drugs under Japan's Act on Securing Quality, Efficacy and Safety of Products Including Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices. One draft revises the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare's designations of certain Class 1 and Class 2 drugs, and the other revises the designation of certain Class 2 drugs requiring special caution. The draft was published on 2026-08-25 with comments accepted until 2026-09-24.

Business Impact

Businesses that manufacture, distribute, or sell affected non-prescription drugs may need to monitor how these classification designations could change handling or sales requirements. The proposals are still at the draft stage and could be modified before any finalization.

Recommended Action

Consider reviewing the official e-Gov public comment notice and the linked draft texts to confirm scope, affected products, and the comment deadline.
