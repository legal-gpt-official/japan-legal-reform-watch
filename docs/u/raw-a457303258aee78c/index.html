Summary

This is a Cabinet Order (Reiwa 8 Cabinet Order No. 276) that adjusts related Cabinet Orders in connection with the partial enforcement of the law to strengthen the pension system in light of socio-economic changes. According to the e-Gov update list, it amends the 2015 Cabinet Order (No. 343) on transitional measures for Employees' Pension Insurance benefits under the pension unification law. The metadata gives a promulgation date of 2026-09-11 and an enforcement date of 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

Employers, payroll and benefits administrators, and pension-related service providers may be affected if the amendments change transitional rules for Employees' Pension Insurance benefits. The scope and practical effect of the changes cannot be determined from the metadata alone.
