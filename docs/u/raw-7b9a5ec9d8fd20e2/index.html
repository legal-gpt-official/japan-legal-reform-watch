摘要

2019年厚生劳动省令第86号修订了《社会保险劳务士法施行规则》（1968年厚生省、劳动省令第1号）。该修订省令配合修订《促进女性在职业生活中发挥作用法》等法律的法律施行，对相关省令进行整备；所载公布日期为2019-12-27，生效日期为2020-06-01。

Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 86 of 2019 amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Labor and Social Security Attorneys Act (Ministries of Health and Welfare and Labour Ordinance No. 1 of 1968). The amending ordinance arranges related ministerial ordinances in connection with implementation of an Act amending the Act on Promotion of Women's Participation and Advancement in the Workplace and other laws; the stated promulgation date is 2019-12-27 and effective date is 2020-06-01.
