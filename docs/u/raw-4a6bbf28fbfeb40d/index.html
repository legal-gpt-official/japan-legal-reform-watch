摘要

2026年厚生劳动省令第144号就《医疗法等部分修正法》的部分施行调整了该省相关省令。所列修订涉及《关于医疗信息基础设施与诊疗报酬审查支付机构在疫情初期确保医疗服务措施相关业务的财务及会计的省令》（2024年厚生劳动省令第6号）。该修订省令于2026-09-30公布，载明的生效日期为2026-10-01。

对企业的影响

医疗信息基础设施与诊疗报酬审查支付机构的疫情初期医疗服务业务所适用的财务及会计规定可能发生变化；所列修订对象的范围小于一般医疗业务。

Summary

Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 144 of 2026 adjusts ministry ordinances in connection with partial implementation of the Act Partially Amending the Medical Care Act and Other Acts. The listed amendment concerns the Ordinance on Finance and Accounting for the Medical Information Infrastructure and Medical Fee Review and Payment Organization's Operations Related to Measures to Secure Medical Care in the Initial Phase of an Epidemic (Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 6 of 2024). The amending ordinance was promulgated on 2026-09-30, with a stated effective date of 2026-10-01.

Business Impact

The Medical Information Infrastructure and Medical Fee Review and Payment Organization may face changes to finance and accounting provisions for its initial-phase epidemic medical-care operations; the listed target is narrower than healthcare operations generally.
