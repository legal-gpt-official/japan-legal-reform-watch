2026-06-05，经济产业省和国土交通省宣布修订《一般海域占用公开招募制度运用指南》。该指南涉及依据《海洋可再生能源发电设施建设法》（2018年第89号法律）遴选海上风电运营商。此次修订经过审议和公开征求意见，旨在确保海上风电投资完成。

Summary

On 2026-06-05, the Ministry of Economy, Trade and Industry and the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism announced revisions to the operational guidelines for public tenders for occupancy of general sea areas. The guidelines concern selection of offshore wind power operators under the Act on Development of Marine Renewable Energy Power Generation Facilities, Act No. 89 of 2018. The revisions followed deliberations and public comment, with the aim of ensuring completion of offshore wind power investment.

Business Impact

Offshore wind power operators participating in these public tenders may be affected by revised operational guidelines intended to support completion of their power generation investments.
