摘要

法务省、厚生劳动省2025年第5号省令于2025-09-30公布，生效日期为2027-04-01。该省令修订了两省的《国家战略特别区域法施行规则》（2015年第1号省令），属于伴随《关于部分修订〈出入国管理及难民认定法〉及〈外国人技能实习适当实施及技能实习生保护法〉的法律》实施而进行的调整及过渡措施的一部分。

Summary

Ministry of Justice and Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare Ordinance No. 5 of 2025 was promulgated on 2025-09-30, with an effective date of 2027-04-01. It amends the ministries' Enforcement Regulations of the National Strategic Special Zones Act, Ordinance No. 1 of 2015, as part of adjustments and transitional measures accompanying implementation of the Act amending the Immigration Control and Refugee Recognition Act and the Act on Proper Technical Intern Training and Protection of Technical Intern Trainees.

Business Impact

Operators subject to the two ministries' National Strategic Special Zones enforcement regulations may be affected by the amendments and transitional measures. The listed amendment concerns those zone-specific regulations, rather than establishing the scope of changes for all employers of foreign workers or technical intern trainees.
