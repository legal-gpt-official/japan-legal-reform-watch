The e-Gov law update list shows a rule (Cabinet Secretariat/Ministry of Defense Order No. 1 of Reiwa 8) concerning side employment of reserve Self-Defense Force personnel, issued under Article 3, Paragraph 1 of the special act on concurrent work by national and local public servants.
