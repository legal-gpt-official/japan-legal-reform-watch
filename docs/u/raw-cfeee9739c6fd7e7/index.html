Summary

This item is a Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications ordinance (Reiwa 8 MIC Ordinance No. 107) that amends the Enforcement Regulations of the Telecommunications Business Act and related rules. According to the metadata, the affected rule concerns the calculation of Type 1 grants and Type 1 contributions related to the provision of the first-category basic telecommunications service (Heisei 14 MIC Ordinance No. 64). The listed promulgation date and effective date are both 2026-08-10.

Business Impact

Entities involved in providing or funding basic telecommunications services may need to consider how changes to grant and contribution calculation rules could affect their obligations. The practical effect could vary depending on each organization's role in the telecommunications sector.
