The Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) has published the results of a public comment procedure on a proposed notice setting amounts to be designated by the Minister under Article 5, Paragraph 1, Item 5(a) of the rules for calculating the Type 1 grant and Type 1 levy related to the provision of Category 1 basic telecommunications services.
