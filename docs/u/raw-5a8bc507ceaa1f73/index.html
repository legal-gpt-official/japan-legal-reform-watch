Summary

This e-Gov item concerns the results of a public comment procedure on draft partial amendments to the Minister of Health, Labour and Welfare's designation of 'designated intractable diseases' and the prescribed severity levels under the Act on Medical Care for Patients with Intractable Diseases, as well as a draft partial revision of the document on diagnostic criteria and severity classification for designated intractable diseases. The metadata lists a draft publication date of 2026/09/28 and a comment deadline of 2026/10/29, and the contact is the Intractable Disease Control Division of the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare. The amendments are described as drafts; the metadata does not state their final content or effective date.

Business Impact

If the amendments are finalized, changes to the list of designated diseases or to diagnostic criteria and severity classifications could affect eligibility for medical expense subsidies and related administrative handling. Healthcare providers, designated medical institutions, and organizations involved in intractable disease care or research may wish to assess whether they are affected.
