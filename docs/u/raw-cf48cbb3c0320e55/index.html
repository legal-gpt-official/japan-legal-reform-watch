Summary

Cabinet Order No. 305 of 2026 adjusts related Cabinet Orders in connection with implementation of the Disaster Management Agency Establishment Act and associated legislation. It amends the Enforcement Order of the Act on Reconstruction from Large-Scale Disasters (Cabinet Order No. 237 of 2013); the listed promulgation date is 2026-09-30 and the effective date is 2026-11-02.
