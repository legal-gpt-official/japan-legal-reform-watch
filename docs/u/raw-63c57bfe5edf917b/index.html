Summary

The Financial Services Agency published an announcement regarding the promulgation of a Cabinet Office Order that partially amends the order prescribing the format of identification credentials and related documents to be carried by staff of the Securities and Exchange Surveillance Commission when conducting inspections and investigations of criminal (offense) cases. According to the announcement, this concerns the format of the identification credentials used by such staff. No further details are provided in the metadata.
